# Record R2: the home page (M2.6)

R2 rebuilt the home page, in Arabic and English, to match the approved artboard
(`docs/design/v2/Main.dc.html`) section by section, on the branch `redesign/r2-home`, which is
`phase-2/redesign` (with R1) plus this. It touches the home page and what only the home page reads:
the home copy, the scan form's island and the showcase's data. The tool pages, the report, the
knowledge pages, the header, the footer and `global.css` are as R1 left them.

## What was built

`components/home/Home.astro` keeps the page's meta, canonical, hreflang and the `WebSite` and
`WebApplication` JSON-LD as they were, over nine sections, each a component:

| Section | Component | Where its words and numbers come from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Hero | `Hero.astro`, with R1's `Aurora.astro` | `HOME[lang].hero`; the tool count from `TOOLS_DATA`; the scope chip's rule count from `LIBRARY_DATA` |
| the scan box | `islands/ScanForm.tsx` in R1's `scan-ring` | `SCAN_FORM`, unchanged |
| 2. Product shot | `Showcase.astro` | `SHOWCASE`: golden report 04, every number |
| 3. Tools marquee | `ToolsMarquee.astro` | sixteen tools by slug from `TOOLS_DATA`: name and category dot |
| 4. Counts | `Counts.astro` | `homeCounts()`: tools, rules, engines, fix guides, glossary terms |
| 5. Bento | `Bento.astro`, `BentoTile.astro` | seven tiles, each linking to its tool page |
| 6. How it works | `HowItWorks.astro` | `HOME[lang].how` |
| 7. Monitoring | `Monitoring.astro` | `HOME[lang].monitoring`, marked «قريباً» |
| 8. Plans | `Plans.astro` | `HOME[lang].plans`, no price |
| 9. FAQ and the closing call | `Faq.astro`, `FinalCta.astro` | the existing FAQ copy |

`SectionIntro.astro` is the centred head (a kicker pill, the heading, a lead) of sections 5 to 9.
Gone: `HeroFigure`, `ScoreScale`, `TopicStrip`, `ArabicLayer`, `ExampleCard`, `Honest` and
`HomeTools`, and the words only they read (the hero's kicker, the figure's old labels, `topics` and
`TOPICS`, `arabic`, `honest`).

Also new: `lib/home.ts` (the counts, the slugs, and the shapes drawn from numbers), `lib/engines.ts`
(the three browsers' names and dots, which the scan form's island uses without a report in its
script), `lettersApart()` in `lib/arabic.ts`, and `SHOWCASE`'s new fields (the rules that did not
apply, the findings of each severity, each scored category, the engines the overflow was seen in
and the third finding).

## The numbers on the page

None is typed in. The counts are the lengths of `tools.json` (44), `library.json` (61), `Engine.options`
(3), `guides.json`'s fix guides (15) and glossary terms (38), read when the site is built; a tool,
rule or guide added tomorrow changes the page with no edit. The product shot is golden report 04:
score 91, 3 failed, 21 passed, 37 not applicable, the severities (1 serious, 1 moderate, 1 info),
the categories (67, 0, 100, 100), the overflow of 280 px on a 390 px screen drawn to scale from the
finding's own box, and 5 declarations of the physical-CSS finding. What is not real data is marked:
six of the seven bento tiles and the monitoring preview say «مثال» / "Example"; the plans carry
no price.

## Decisions

1. **One form.** The closing call to action links to the scan box (`#scan`) instead of holding a
   second form, so the page has one `<form>`, one `home-url`, one Turnstile. The island lost its
   `tone` prop and gained two (`scope`, `enginesLabel`); its logic, ids, names, notes and error are
   as they were, and so is its hydration (`client:idle`).
2. **The page's CSP allows no style attribute**, so every shape that comes from a number is SVG,
   whose sizes are attributes: the ring (`pathLength="100"`, so the dash is the score), the bars (an
   `x` and a `width` in percent, from the side the page starts on), the gauges, the overflow drawing
   and the monitoring chart. Measured on the built pages in all three engines: no `style` attribute
   and no CSP violation, after hydration.
3. **Motion is CSS, and each animation has its own off-switch.** Fifteen run (the aurora, the ring,
   the floating cards, the bars, the marquee, the letters parting, the typed name with its caret and
   its «accepted», the arrow, the gauges, the beam, the live dot); with `prefers-reduced-motion` none
   runs, in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit, read from `document.getAnimations()`. Under reduced motion
   the WebKit row stays drawn apart (it is what the tile is about) and the marquee is a row that wraps.
4. **The count-up is a 579-byte inline script**, hashed by the build. The number is in the HTML as
   it stands; the script counts it up once, from 80 px before it comes into view, and does nothing
   with reduced motion, without `IntersectionObserver` or without script. The ring's score does not
   count: its arc draws itself in CSS.
5. **The letters parting** is the artboard's technique, kept: a zero-width inline-block between the
   letters, with zero-width joiners on both sides so each letter is drawn in its joined shape, as when
   a browser applies letter-spacing it should not. `lettersApart()` produces the pieces from the
   golden page's heading; `drawnWithLetterSpacing()` is now those pieces with a fixed gap, and writes
   the string it always did. Probed in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit: the gaps open in all three. The
   visual is `aria-hidden` with the plain text for a screen reader beside it, and on a phone the word
   has its own line so it never breaks inside.
6. **Weights.** Only 400 and 600, as R1's budget: the artboard's bold headings are semibold, and
   its 500 chips are regular (`chip font-normal`: R1's utility sets 500, and a utility with a later
   first property wins). The wa.me strings are regular so the page does not ask for Plex Mono 600.
   Every text node on the page, in all three engines, is 400 or 600 except the wordmark, which is
   DM Sans 700 as R1 left it.
7. **Tile = one link.** A bento tile's heading is the link, stretched over the tile with a pseudo-element
   (nothing nested); a screen reader hears the tool's name after the heading; the focus ring is
   drawn around the tile with `has-[a:focus-visible]`.
8. **The marquee is a list**: the names once for a screen reader, the copy that makes the loop
   `aria-hidden`, no link in it (nothing moves under a keyboard). The loop has no seam: the track has
   `padding-inline-end` of one gap, which makes half of it exactly one copy; measured, the distance
   between the copies equals half the track in both directions.
9. **The scan form.** Its label is visually hidden, as the artboard has it, and stays the field's
   label; the focus ring is drawn round the whole field row; the `role="alert"` paragraph is always
   rendered, and spaced by a margin only when it has a message, so the empty one adds no gap. The
   engines are a list with its own label; the scope chip, which is not an engine, is not in it.
10. **Everything below the first screen is `below-fold`** (R1's `content-visibility: auto`).
11. **Named breakpoints only.** Tailwind sorts `min-[1100px]:` before `sm:`, so `sm:grid-cols-2` beat
    `min-[1100px]:grid-cols-4` and the bento stayed at two columns at 1440 px. The page uses `sm`,
    `md`, `lg` and `xl`.

### What R3 to R5 should know

- **axe reads a glow or an aurora blob as the solid colour it is under the blur.** The dark band's
  quiet text (`panel-dim`, #a9b6c8) measured 4.14:1 on the indigo glow's composite (#42458c), which
  lowered Lighthouse's phone accessibility to 97 and would have failed the gate; `panel-soft`
  (#d5deea) is 6.25:1 there. Text over a glow or a blob must pass on the unblurred composite.
  Lighthouse's phone run, not the desktop one, is where it showed (the glass sat over the glow only in
  the one-column layout).
- Gradients, translucent glass over the aurora's grid, the scan ring's pseudo-element and text with a
  gradient fill are `incomplete` to axe (reported for review, never a failure): 33 to 63 nodes
  on a home page, depending on the width, none a violation. The full axe ruleset (WCAG 2.2 AA and best practice) found
  no violation on either page at 390 px and 1280 px.
- Arablyzer's own `rtl-physical-css` flags `left`/`right`, `text-align: left|right` and the physical
  margins, paddings and radii in any stylesheet, `<style>` included: the page has none (logical
  properties and `inset-inline-*` throughout); `translate`, `clip-path: inset()` and SVG attributes are
  not flagged.
- The shared Tailwind output (inlined in every page) grew by about 10 KB raw (2 KB gzipped) with this
  page's utilities. Styles used by one component only are in its `<style>` block (scoped, inlined,
  hashed), so only the home page pays for them.

## Where R2 departs from the artboard

1. **Headings semibold and chips regular**, not bold and medium (the weight budget, above).
2. **The heading's words** are the existing copy («والزبون العربي على جواله»), not the artboard's.
3. **The tool chips link to the tool pages**, as the brief says; the artboard's buttons fill the box
   with a tool, a design for the scan flow that does not exist.
4. **The product shot's summary line** is the report's own tally («فشلت 3 قواعد · نجحت 21 قاعدة»),
   not «مشكلتان وملاحظة»; the severity words are the site's own («خطير»), not the artboard's feminine
   «خطيرة»; its address pill says it is one of our test pages and names it (`04-rtl-layout`), instead of
   `example.com`.
5. **The first glass card** sits above the window's corner and says less («انفصلت حروف العنوان»), because
   the artboard's position hid the score once the English words are longer; the three cards show from
   1280 px (`xl`), not 1100 px, and are hidden from a screen reader, since the window says the same.
6. **The WhatsApp and robots.txt tiles are marked «مثال»**; the artboard marks four. The engines tile is
   the only one that is real data, so the only one unmarked.
7. **Plans: no price.** The artboard's «0 للأبد», «[السعر]» and «حسب الحاجة» are not on the page. The cards are
   named for what they do («مجاني»، «مراقبة يومية»، «زحف المواقع الكاملة»), not «المحترف» and «الشركات», which
   the owner has not chosen, and each paid card says «قريباً» where the artboard has a button. The
   gradient edge is on the free plan, the only one that can be used, not on the pro one. The paid
   plans promise what the FAQ already does and nothing the plan's other features suggest.
8. **The monitoring band** has a «قريباً» pill, no link to a dashboard (the artboard's «شاهد لوحة التحكم»), the
   future tense, and a fourth point from the plan (§5.6, what changed between two scans) in place of
   «فريقك كله في حساب واحد», which no document promises. Its preview says «بيانات مثال».
9. **The counts' last card spans two columns on a phone**, so five cards do not leave one alone.
10. **The closing call is a link**, not a button to a page.
11. **The marquee names sixteen of the 44 tools**, the artboard's own list, from the registry; all 44 in a
    row that wraps for reduced motion would be twenty lines on a phone.
12. **On a phone the engines tile stacks**: the engine and its verdict on a line, the word under them.
13. **Breakpoints** are Tailwind's named ones (1024 and 1280 px) where the artboard's are 1100 and 1180.
