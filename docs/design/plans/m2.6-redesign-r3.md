# M2.6 R3: the tool pages and the tools index (Record R3)

R3, the tool pages and the tools index, was built on 2026-10-02 on the branch `redesign/r3-tools`,
from `phase-2/redesign` (R1 in it). It touches the 44 tool pages in both languages, the directory
(`/tools`, `/en/tools`) and the islands under them. The home page, the report, the knowledge pages,
the header, the footer and `global.css` are as R1 left them; R2, R4 and R5 work in their own
branches.

## 1. What was built

### The tool page (`components/tools/ToolPage.astro`)

Top to bottom, in the artboard's order, in v2:

1. **The hero**, a `div` (the site audit wants no `<section>` and no `<h2>` before the tool's form):
   the breadcrumb (Tools, then the category with its dot), the `<h1>` in the gradient, the lead
   (a `<p>`, right after the `<h1>`, as the audit reads it), and the tool in its box. A still wash
   of two radial gradients stands behind it, capped at 34 rem of height; the aurora is the home
   page's.
2. **The sections**, in a centred 880 px column, each an icon on its tint beside its heading
   (`ToolSection.astro`):
   - `#checks`: «ماذا تفحص» as a card of green ticks.
   - `#example`: «wrong» and «right» side by side, a pill over a code panel on the navy ground.
   - `#fix`: the fix, a card of prose; its code blocks are the navy ones of `prose-fix`.
   - `#faq`: the questions as accordions.
   - `#links`: «ما تشغّله هذه الأداة» (a card of rows: the rule's id in `<code>`, its name, its
     severity) and «أدوات قريبة» (cards, each with its icon, name and question).
   - `#about`: «كيف نقيس», the methodology as one more accordion, with the date of the last update
     and the links to the methodology and to the full check.

### The box (`islands/ToolBox.tsx`)

The home page's scan box, shared by the five islands: `scan-glow` under a `scan-ring` around a white
box of 24 px of radius, and in it the **pill** of the tool (its category's dot, its name; a scan
tool has a × that leaves for the home page's full check, `/#scan`). A check in progress puts
`reading-beam` on the box. `FIELD`, `AREA`, `LABEL`, `SECONDARY` and `SUBMIT` are the classes of the
fields and buttons of the generators and the paste tool.

- **`ToolApp`** (the 40 scan tools): the address as the artboard's large borderless row with a link
  icon (its focus ring is the row's, inside the box), then the browsers or what the tool reads, and
  the gradient button (`btn-grad btn-lg`, full width on a phone). The notes sit under the box and the
  error in it. The result is a `card`: a mark (a tick, a note, a warning) or the severity pill
  before its headline, the findings, the list of what was checked with a tinted tile for each
  state, and the footer's links.
- **The generators and the paste tool**: fields with the `field` border colour (3:1) at 48 px; the
  output is a gradient panel holding white boxes, each with a copy button (`btn-grad`, 36 px); the
  robots.txt tester's answer is a card with a coloured edge.

Every id, `name`, label, ARIA wire and the markup the tests and the audit read is as it was:
`#tool-url`, `#tool-note`, `#tool-query-note`, `#tool-error`, `#result-title`,
`[aria-labelledby=result-checked] li` (its last child the status, its green tick an
`svg.text-pass`), `[aria-labelledby=result-notices] [role=note]`, `article > header > span`,
`a[href=#fix]`, `p[role=status]`, `form[data-tool-kind]`, `section[aria-label] pre`, `#wa-*`,
`#href-*`, `#code-*`, `#x-default`, `#product-*`, `#robots-*`.

### The directory (`components/tools/ToolsIndex.astro`)

The hero (the title in the gradient, the lead, the search box with the field's border colour, the
count), a **chip** for each category under it (a `button` that is pressed or not: `aria-pressed`;
Enter and Space), and a section for each category (its icon, name and line) over a grid of

as sent. On a phone the chips are one row that scrolls sideways (fifteen of them would be six
lines); the Arabic layer comes first, named beside its chips. The sidebar of categories is gone:
the chips are what it was.

## 2. Where R3 departs from the brief and the artboard

1. **The three browsers are on the tools that open the page in browsers**, 14 of the 40 scan tools. The artboard
   shows them on one tool that does. A tool that reads the HTML as the server sends it, the
   robots.txt, DNS records, the links, the sitemaps or Chrome's data opens no browser, and its chip
   says what it reads («HTML», «robots.txt», «DNS», …). The three generators and the paste tool have
   no such row.
2. **«كيف نقيس» comes after «أدوات قريبة», not before.** The brief lists the methodology first, as
   the artboard's FAQ ends with it. The site audit (BUILD-PLAN §6.1) wants `#links` before `#about`
   in the page, and `#links` holds the related tools: the order is the audit's. A visual order
   other than the document's would also have put the keyboard's order out of the eye's.
3. **No «rendered preview» in the example.** The artboard draws, for the letter-spacing tool, the
   word as WebKit draws it. An example in the tools' copy is code, of five kinds (HTML, robots.txt,
   HTTP, DNS, JSON), and no data says how it looks. Drawing one by hand for 44 tools is inventing it.
   What is there: the code panel with each value that makes the example wrong (orange) or right
   (teal) marked with a tint of its colour, where the artboard marks the line. It is the value, not
   the line: the highlighter marks values, and cutting highlighted HTML at a line break is fragile.
   A later milestone could add an optional `preview` to the copy format of `packages/tools` for the
   tools of the Arabic layer.
4. **The accordions are a heading that holds a button**, not `<details>`. The audit's FAQ check wants
   each question an `<h3>` followed by its answer, and a heading inside a `<summary>` is followed by
   nothing. They are in the HTML open (the buttons say `aria-expanded="true"`) and a script of the
   page folds them, so a crawler and a visitor without script read every answer. The methodology
   is the same widget, at `h2`.
5. **`chip` is drawn in regular weight on the directory.** R1's `chip` utility is 500, a 45 KB file of
   Arabic a page does not otherwise load (see the record of R1); the directory sets `font-normal!`.
6. **The whole title is the gradient**, in an inline `<span>` (the clip needs the inline box, not the
   line box, or an Arabic glyph taller than the line is cut), with the Latin words in the title, the
   lead and the cards isolated for the bidi algorithm (`Bidi.astro`).
7. **The breadcrumb is `ink-2`**, not `ink-3`: over the wash, the grey `ink-3` measured 4.43:1 on the
   directory's breadcrumb at 390 px (pixels of a screenshot with the text hidden), under the 4.5
   it is held to; `ink-2` measures 6.85. The tool page's wash is capped at 34 rem of height, so it
   does not follow the result of a scan down to the notes under the box.

## 3. What the site's own checks taught

- `rtl-physical-css` is information, yet it fails the self-scan: a rule that finds something is
  `fail` whatever its severity. A `text-left` in the source makes Tailwind emit it in the Arabic
  page's stylesheet, used or not, and two declarations were enough. The address field's text is
  `text-end` in Arabic and `text-start` in English, which are logical.
- The tool's form must come before the first `<section>` or `<h2>` in the page, and the H1 must be
  followed by its `<p>`: nothing in the hero may be a section or a heading.
- The tests read `section[aria-label]` as the one result of a generator: no other section of a tool
  page may carry an `aria-label` (the sections use `aria-labelledby`).
- A focus ring drawn outside a button that fills its card is cut by the card's `overflow-hidden`: the
  accordions' buttons draw it inside (`outline-offset: -3px`).
- At 320 px the card's padding made a `code.whole` of the robots.txt tester's fix wider than the card,
  and the page scrolled sideways by 7 px; below 360 px it may break.
