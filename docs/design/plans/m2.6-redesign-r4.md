# M2.6 R4: the report and the scan progress (Record R4)

R4 was built on 2026-10-02 on the branch `redesign/r4-report`, from `phase-2/redesign` (R1: the v2
tokens, utilities, fonts, header and footer). It restyles the report island, its progress view and
its states in the v2 look, on the structure of the approved canvas's «التقرير» and «أثناء الفحص»
artboards (Report.dc.html and Scan.dc.html of the canvas, v1 in layout): the page is a thread under
the address that was scanned. The data model, the states, the routing and the report page's head
(`noindex`, no canonical) are as they were; `ReportPage.astro` changes only in its no-script note.

## What changed

### The thread (`islands/report/Thread.tsx`)

- The address the visitor asked for is a bubble at the end of the line: the teal-to-indigo gradient,
  white text (5.68:1 at the teal end), the address a link to the page when the scan is over
  (`rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener"`, as before) and breaking anywhere, so a 300-character
  address wraps inside the bubble. Under it, small chips: the day it was scanned, the page's HTTP
  status, the rules' version, and for a tool's result the tool, linked.
- Arablyzer's answer sits under it, with its mark (the logo's gradient square) at the start of the
  line, and above the answer on a phone, so the cards take the phone's whole width.
- Every state is drawn in it: the report, the scan under way, and the five states that are not a
  report (blocked, opted out, failed, not found, unreachable).

### The finished report (`ReportView.tsx`, `ReadLine.tsx`, `Summary.tsx`, `Findings.tsx`)

- **What was read.** One line, a button: «قرأنا الصفحة في Chromium وFirefox وWebKit، وشغّلنا 61
  قاعدة» (the engines that rendered, and the rules that ran). It opens on the steps the scan took
  as the report tells them (robots.txt's status, the page's status and type, real visitors' data
  when the report holds it, how many browsers rendered, the rules) and, under the browsers, a card
  each with its version, how it went and how many requests the page made. The browser that shows a
  problem none of the others shows is the one with the serious edge, as before.
- **Partial note and notices**: amber, `role="note"`, each notice with its icon. The notices keep
  their section and their screen-reader heading (the tool page's result reads them the same way).
- **Summary.** The score as a ring that draws itself in the teal-to-indigo gradient, its number in
  the same gradient; the headline, which is the page's `h1`, counting problems and notes from the
  rules' results («مشكلتان وملاحظة واحدة في هذه الصفحة»); the severity pills with their counts; how
  many checks passed and did not apply; the CLI's score caption when fewer rules ran or the scan is
  partial; the methodology link. A tool's result has no score, so no ring and no categories.
- **Categories.** A bar for each that scored under 100, lowest first, coloured by its band (90 and
  50, the cut-offs Lighthouse draws; the bar and the number are the score), growing from the start
  of the line; then one line naming the categories at 100 («درجتها 100») and one naming those no
  rule applied to.
- **Findings.** An `h2`, the severity filter (kept, `aria-pressed`) and one accordion for each rule
  that failed or needs a review, the most severe first and open: its head holds the severity, the
  title, the category and, when it applies, «في WebKit وحده», «لا تُخصم من الدرجة» and «تحتاج عين
  إنسان»; its body, each finding's message with its evidence, «و17 غيرها», the fix in a tinted
  panel, and the link to the rule's page with its id. The head is a button inside the heading,
  with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`; Enter and Space work; a closed panel is `hidden`.
- **Checks.** The rules that passed, those that did not apply and, new, those that could not run
  (the CLI's words, from `STRINGS`), each a fold of the same kind, with the rule's link and id.
- **Actions.** Scan again (the gradient button, to the home form or the tool's page, with the
  address), copy the link, the JSON, and the line that says the report is not in search engines.
- **The dock** (`Dock.tsx`), sticky at the foot of the page: a field for another address that
  starts the scan as the home page's form does (`precheck`, Turnstile, `startScan`) and opens its
  page; a tool's result goes on with that tool. The dock lets clicks through but for its box, and
  `html:has(.report-dock)` gets `scroll-padding-block-end`, so a control focused behind it scrolls
  clear.

### The scan under way (`Progress.tsx`, `Steps.tsx`, `Engines.tsx`, `Dock.tsx`)

The address in its bubble, the `h1`, the queue (still a status that starts empty), the steps with
a line between them (done in a gradient disc, the one under way a ring that turns, the rest to
come; what each read beside its name; the states still said to a screen reader), the browsers as
chips that say what each is doing, the log (kept, focusable), the shape of the report in quiet
blocks, and over the dock the scan box: the address in a white box inside the rotating ring
(`scan-ring`), the reading beam over it (`reading-beam`, right to left in Arabic, left to right in
English), the step it is on, «الخطوة 4 من 6», and a track. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the beam
is not drawn, the ring stands still and the spinner's ring is drawn whole in the brand's colour;
read in Chromium and Firefox from the computed styles with the preference on and off.

### Evidence (`Evidence.tsx`)

The same evidence, in the v2 look: the diagram (still to scale, from the finding's box, `overflow`
and `viewportWidth`) on a tinted panel with a rounded screen, cropped to what it draws so that a
phone shows its labels at a readable size (its geometry is `diagramOf`, in the model); the selector
in its own box, which wraps; the engines that saw it, as chips with their colour; the code on the
navy panel, focusable, scrolling inside its block. On a phone the labels sit over their values.

### The model (`report-model.ts`)

`summaryCounts`, `headlineOf`, `bandOf`, `categoryRows`, `renderedEngines`, `aloneEngines`,
`onlyEngine`, `listOf`, `checklistOf`, `diagramOf`, and `idFromPath`, moved here from `ReportApp`
so a unit test imports no island. The headline counts rules, as the report's own `summary` does
(`summaryCounts(report).bySeverity` equals `report.summary.bySeverity` on three golden reports).

### Copy (`packages/i18n/src/report.ts`, `reviewed: false`)

A new group, `thread`: the bubble's verb, the read line, the summary (the headline, with the dual
and the plurals of each language, the checks sentence, «من 100»), the category and findings
headings, «في {browser} وحده», «عن هذه القاعدة», the dock and the step count. Gone: `header.title`,
`contents` but its `engines`, `results`, `noRules`, `tabs` but its `filter`, `score.title`,
`score.categories` and `findings.title`, which only the Lab layout read; `header.kicker` stays for
the Open Graph card. The words that count are tested in each language
(`packages/i18n/test/copy.test.ts`).

### CSS (`global.css`, the R4 block at the end)

`ring-draw` and `bar-grow` (each stopped under reduced motion), `bar-good`, `bar-mid`, `bar-low`
(with forced-colours rules), and `html:has(.report-dock)`. In forced-colours mode (emulated in
Chromium) the bars take the highlight colour, the ring the system's, and the bubble, the notices,
the tinted panels, the tile of a state and the scan box keep an edge, since a palette takes their
fills away. No `style` attribute in any Astro
markup; the island sets the bars' and the track's widths from script, after it has run (the shell
the server sends has none, and a browser test says so). Only weights 400 and 600: the computed
`font-weight` of every text node in `main`, in nine scenarios in both languages, is one of the two.

## Where R4 departs from the canvas

1. No «راقب هذه الصفحة كل يوم» band: monitoring is Phase 4, and R2's plans band is where it is
   announced.
2. The line under the bubble is not «كل الفحوص، 61 قاعدة» (the read line says what ran); it holds
   the date, the status and the rules' version, which the old header showed.
3. The categories at 100 are named «درجتها 100», not «بلا مشاكل»: a score over the rules that ran is
   not a promise that none failed to run.
4. The amber of the middle band starts at #D97706, not #F59E0B (3.19:1 against white, and the end
   colour, where the bar meets its track, is 4.29:1 or better in each band).
5. The old sidebar (the score panel and the contents list) and the tabs are gone, the thread being
   one column; the severity filter and every number they showed stay.
6. The canvas's dock links to the scan page; here it starts the scan.
7. The steps of a finished scan are those its report holds: a step it holds nothing of (robots.txt
   without its fact, real visitors' data without CrUX's) is not listed, instead of «لم تعمل».

## Bugs found on the way

- **The evidence's diagram never had its dashes, its widths or its anchors.** Preact does not turn
  `strokeWidth`, `strokeDasharray`, `textAnchor` and `fontSize` into the names SVG reads, so the
  screen was a one-pixel outline, the box solid and the labels drawn from their left edge. They are
  written as SVG writes them (`stroke-width`, …). The home page's diagram is Astro markup and was
  never affected.
- **WebKit's Arabic `Intl.ListFormat` wraps each name in U+2068 and U+2069**, which the page named
  «⟨U+2068⟩» on Safari. The engines are listed by a function written out.
- The report test imported `idFromPath` from `ReportApp`, which now reaches `astro:env`: it lives
  in the model.
