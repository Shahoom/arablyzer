# M5.6 WhatsApp alerts — Implementation Plan

> **ملخص للمالك:** تنبيهات واتساب جاهزة ومطفأة: لا تعمل حتى تضبط `ARABLYZER_WHATSAPP_PROVIDER=cloud` مع `WHATSAPP_PHONE_NUMBER_ID` و`WHATSAPP_ACCESS_TOKEN`، وتوافق Meta على **خمسة قوالب** نصّها في هذا الملف (أربعة من فئة «خدمة» وواحد «مصادقة»). الشخص يضيف رقمه في `/account`، فيصله رمز تأكيد على واتساب، وبعد التأكيد يفعّل واتساب **لكل موقع على حدة** (هذا هو الموافقة). لا نرسل إلا القوالب المعتمدة، ولا نرسل الملخص الأسبوعي، ولا نرسل إلى رقم لم يؤكَّد أو موقع لم يُفعَّل. ردّ الشخص بكلمة «إيقاف»/STOP يحذف رقمه (إن ضبطت webhook). التنبيهات خاصية خطة: الخطة المجانية بلا واتساب (كما في §3 من الخطة) إلا إن شغّلتها بـ`ARABLYZER_PLAN_ACCOUNT_WHATSAPP_ALERTS=on` لتجرّبها بحسابك. **ينتظرك:** حساب Meta Business موثّق، ورقم مخصّص، وإنشاء القوالب أدناه والموافقة عليها، ورمز وصول دائم، وقراءة النص العربي (`reviewed: false`).

**Goal:** A signed-in account whose plan has WhatsApp alerts can confirm a WhatsApp number, opt monitored sites in one by one, and get the monitor's urgent alerts there through the WhatsApp Cloud API. Off until the owner sets the provider; no account exists yet, so every call is tested against a stand-in.

Built on `m5.5-email-alerts.md` (the per-channel delivery ledger, `Mailer`'s outcomes) and `m4.3-monitoring.md`.

## Design

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Provider | WhatsApp Cloud API: `POST https://graph.facebook.com/<version>/<phone-number-id>/messages` with a template, through `safeFetch` and the egress proxy. Off while `ARABLYZER_WHATSAPP_PROVIDER` is empty. On needs `WHATSAPP_PHONE_NUMBER_ID` (digits) and `WHATSAPP_ACCESS_TOKEN`; the API and the monitor refuse to start with half of it. `WHATSAPP_API_VERSION` is optional (default `v23.0`) |
| Templates only | A business-initiated message needs an approved template, so nothing is free text. Five templates (below); the variables are cleaned to one line of at most 200 characters (Meta refuses line breaks and long runs), so a scanned site's text cannot break or extend a message |
| Consent | Two steps, both the person's: **(1)** a number is saved unverified and a six-digit code (valid 10 minutes, 5 tries, then locked; stored as an HMAC under the sending token, never in the clear) is sent to it as an authentication template; the number is usable only after the code comes back. **(2)** each monitored site is opted in on its own (`whatsapp_sites`); turning monitoring off ends it. Nothing is sent to an unverified number or an un-opted site |
| Abuse and cost | A plan setting `whatsappAlerts` (env `ARABLYZER_PLAN_<PLAN>_WHATSAPP_ALERTS`, default off for the free account, inherited and never lowered by a paid plan) gates code requests and sends. Codes are limited to 3 an hour per person, 3 an hour per number (keyed by a hash, across accounts) and 120 an hour for the server. Each template sent is a charge on the owner's Meta account |
| What goes out | One template per event of a run: score drop, new critical findings, site cannot be scanned, monitoring stopped. Never the weekly summary. The person's own thresholds and switches decide the events, as for the webhook and the mail |
| No double send | Each template is recorded per run in the delivery ledger (`whatsapp:score-drop` …); a retry (1 min … 6 h) sends only what has not gone, and the other channels are not repeated. A refusal for good (a number not on WhatsApp, a template not approved or with wrong variables) is counted as handled and not retried; Meta's "too fast" codes, 5xx, 401/403 and no answer are retried, then given up. A provider that throws never reaches the webhook, the mail or the run |
| STOP | With `WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET` and `WHATSAPP_VERIFY_TOKEN` (set together) the API serves Meta's webhook at `/api/whatsapp/webhook`: the GET check answers the challenge for the verify token; a POST is believed only with a valid `X-Hub-Signature-256` over the raw body; a text of "stop"/"إيقاف"/… from a number forgets that number (every account that saved it) and its opt-ins. Delivery statuses and other messages are ignored |
| Privacy | The number is kept only while the account keeps it, goes with the account (cascade) and with STOP or "Remove the number". The privacy page now names it (once confirmed) and lists Resend and Meta as processors (`packages/i18n/src/legal.ts`) |

## The templates (to create in WhatsApp Manager → Message templates)

Names are exact. Create each in **Arabic (`ar`)** and **English (`en`)** under the same name. Categories: the first four **Utility**, the last **Authentication**. Add the sample values shown when Meta asks for examples. Variables never start or end a body, as Meta requires.

**1. `arablyzer_score_drop`** (Utility), variables: site, old score, new score, report link.
- ar: `تنبيه من Arablyzer: انخفضت درجة الموقع {{1}} من {{2}} إلى {{3}}. افتح التقرير: {{4}} — فريق Arablyzer`
- en: `Arablyzer alert: the score of {{1}} dropped from {{2}} to {{3}}. Open the report: {{4}} — the Arablyzer team`
- samples: `example.com`, `92`, `74`, `https://arablyzer.com/r/AbC123xyz`

**2. `arablyzer_critical`** (Utility), variables: site, count, report link.
- ar: `تنبيه من Arablyzer: ظهرت مشاكل حرجة جديدة في الموقع {{1}}، وعددها {{2}}. افتح التقرير: {{3}} — فريق Arablyzer`
- en: `Arablyzer alert: new critical findings appeared on {{1}}, {{2}} in all. Open the report: {{3}} — the Arablyzer team`
- samples: `example.com`, `2`, `https://arablyzer.com/r/AbC123xyz`

**3. `arablyzer_site_down`** (Utility), variable: site.
- ar: `تنبيه من Arablyzer: تعذّر فحص الموقع {{1}} وقد يكون متوقفاً. سنحاول مرة أخرى في الفحص التالي. — فريق Arablyzer`
- en: `Arablyzer alert: {{1}} could not be scanned and may be down. We will try again at the next scan. — the Arablyzer team`
- sample: `example.com`

**4. `arablyzer_monitor_paused`** (Utility), variables: site, account page link.
- ar: `تنبيه من Arablyzer: أوقفنا مراقبة الموقع {{1}} بعد محاولات فاشلة متتالية. لتشغيلها من جديد افتح صفحة حسابك: {{2}} — فريق Arablyzer`
- en: `Arablyzer alert: we stopped monitoring {{1}} after repeated failed scans. To turn it on again, open your account page: {{2}} — the Arablyzer team`
- samples: `example.com`, `https://arablyzer.com/account`

**5. `arablyzer_verify`** (Authentication). Choose **Copy code** as the button, add the security recommendation, and a 10-minute expiry. Meta writes the text ("{{1}} is your verification code"); the code is sent in the body and on the button, as Meta's authentication templates require.

## Tests

Numbers (Arabic-Indic digits, `00`, spaces, refusals), masking, template variables; settings from the environment (off, complete, each missing value stops the start, the webhook pair); the Cloud API with a stand-in (payload for each template, the copy button on the code, every status and Meta code → sent / retry / rejected, a throwing or silent provider, the log never holds the token or a number); routes (off without a provider or a plan, code sent and verified, masked number, wrong/locked/expired code, per-person/per-number/server limits, nothing stored when the provider refuses, guards, site opt-in needs a monitor and a verified number, ends with monitoring, another person's 404); Meta's webhook (challenge, signature, STOP in both languages, non-text and foreign numbers ignored, not mounted without its two values); scheduler (a template per event in the account's language, nothing when the plan/number/site says no, never the summary, no double send in a retry, a refusal is not retried, a throwing provider changes nothing else); the PostgreSQL contract (run locally against PostgreSQL 16); the account page in Chromium (hidden when off; code, wrong code, confirmed, site opt-in, remove).

## Not tested without real accounts

Everything that touches Meta: that the five templates are approved as written (Meta may re-categorise or ask for changes), that the request shape is accepted for a real number, that the Arabic template renders right to left, that the access token has the right permission, Meta's webhook check and signature from Meta's side, the price per message, and a real phone receiving a code or an alert.

## Not in M5.6

Free-text replies and conversations (any reply but STOP is ignored); delivery-status handling (a message Meta accepts and later cannot deliver is not noticed); WhatsApp for the weekly summary; a per-event choice of channel; a second sending number; marketing messages; the owner-panel override of the plan setting (`ARABLYZER_PLAN_ACCOUNT_WHATSAPP_ALERTS` is the switch until paid plans exist).

## For the owner

1. A Meta Business account, verified, with a WhatsApp Business account and a number that is not used in the WhatsApp app; its **phone number id**.
2. Create the five templates above and wait for approval (usually minutes to a day).
3. A system user with the `whatsapp_business_messaging` permission, its permanent token, and a payment method (Meta bills the template messages).
4. Set `ARABLYZER_WHATSAPP_PROVIDER=cloud`, `WHATSAPP_PHONE_NUMBER_ID`, `WHATSAPP_ACCESS_TOKEN`. For STOP: in the app's WhatsApp configuration set the callback URL to `https://<the site>/api/whatsapp/webhook`, a verify token of your choice (the same value as `WHATSAPP_VERIFY_TOKEN`), subscribe to the `messages` field, and set `WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET` to the app's secret.
5. To try it yourself before paid plans exist: `ARABLYZER_PLAN_ACCOUNT_WHATSAPP_ALERTS=on`, then add your number on `/account`.
6. Read the Arabic (`reviewed: false`) and the privacy page's two new lines.
