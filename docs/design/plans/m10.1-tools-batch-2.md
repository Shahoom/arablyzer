## 2. Decisions

- **The slug rule (#27).** It reads the page's address and the canonical it names, one path segment at a time, as people read it (percent-encoding undone). It flags harakat and tatweel (`marks`), Eastern Arabic digits (`digits`), an underscore, a space, a plus sign, a left-over percent sign, a capital Latin letter, a double hyphen or punctuation (`separators`), and a last segment over 100 characters (`long`, our threshold: Google gives no number). A clean Arabic slug, encoded or not, passes: Google reads UTF-8 addresses. The generator writes what the rule passes (a test runs the rule over what the generator writes), and the generator's page can show its live example because the example is a canonical the rule judges. The code is `lib/url-slug.ts`, exported as `@arablyzer/rules/url-slug` for the generator.
- **Store tools are lenses with the platform's own steps.** Salla, Zid and Shopify pages are judged by the store rules that apply to any store. What a platform adds is (a) one rule of its own where we can verify its markup (Zid's default title and description were read on `demo.zid.store`; Shopify writes `Shopify.locale` in its storefront script), and (b) the steps to fix, which come from each platform's help center and developer documentation. **Salla has no rule of its own**: its demo store was not reachable (404), so we had no page of a Salla store to read. `PLATFORM_FIXES` gained the steps for `url-slug-invalid` on the three platforms and for the two platform rules.
- **A right fixture may show the platform.** A page of a store platform is reported by `platform-detected` (information), so the right fixture of a platform rule cannot fail nothing. `expect.json` of a right fixture may now list information-only rules (`completeness.test.ts` checks that they are `info`; `fixtures.test.ts` expects exactly them).
- **A page that talks about a mix is not a mix.** The self-audit runs the rules on our own pages, and a rule page that explains «ر.س» beside «SAR» is such a page. The three locale rules leave text between guillemets or curly quotes («…»): a quoted figure is an example, not what the page states. Code was already left out.
- **One island for the text tools.** `TextTool.tsx` renders any generator or paste tool made of a form and an answer; each tool is a small spec in `apps/web/src/islands/generators/text-tools/` (fields, a pure `run`), its words in `packages/i18n/src/text-tools.ts`, its logic in `packages/generators`. The next batches' generators use it.
- **The error decoder knows 26 messages**, written from Google's own help pages for the page indexing, sitemap, structured data, Merchant Center and Core Web Vitals reports. The Arabic interface's wording is matched by stems and was **not** checked against the live Arabic interface; a message it does not know gets the list of those it does.

## 3. Pitfalls met

- A rule that needs a store platform cannot have a right fixture that fails nothing (above).
- The site's self-audit (`pnpm site:audit`, run locally on a built site) found the new rules failing our own rule and tool pages, and the new text fields missing `autocomplete`. Run it after a build and the og images (`astro build` removes `dist/og`).
- A tool's right example must make every rule the tool lists apply and pass: `platform-detected` cannot be listed by a tool with other rules, because it reports on the right example.
- The example of a tool that shows structured data must be a page, not a fragment of JSON; the example of a rule must be a contiguous excerpt of its fixtures.
