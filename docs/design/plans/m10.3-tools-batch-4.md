## 2. Decisions

- **The FAQ rule compares letters, not layout.** The page puts a space where its markup has two nodes side by side, and a question can hold a code sample, so the visible text (code included, scripts and styles not) and the markup's text are compared with every space taken out; an answer by its first 50 letters. The site's own FAQ markup (`faqPage`, plain text of the shown HTML) passes it on all our pages.
- **A page's markup for content the page no longer shows is now a finding on our own pages too**: three cases of `packages/seo/test/audit.test.ts` (no FAQ, an empty question, a hidden FAQ section) now also fail `own-rules`, which is what the rule is for.
- **`markup-content-mismatch`** looks in the visible text for the price of the product the page describes of itself, its rating (value or count), the names of a breadcrumb trail and the last eight digits of a `telephone`. Every number in the text is read in either script and both ways round (`12,500` as 12500 and as 12.5), so a page showing «١٢٠ ر.س» matches a price of 120 and an Omani price in three decimals matches. The fixtures of `breadcrumb-schema-invalid`, `local-business-invalid` and `product-rating-invalid` gained the visible text their markup describes.
- **`og-locale-mismatch`** compares the language part of `og:locale` with `<html lang>`, and flags a hyphen in the locale (Open Graph writes `ar_SA`). It judges nothing without both.
- **`image-alt-quality`** flags three kinds of alt text that do not describe the image: a file name, a word that only names the kind, and a description that begins by saying it is a picture. An empty `alt=""` is right for a decoration and a missing alt is the accessibility checks'. The pattern lists are short and ours.
- **The local business generator** writes names as `name` and `alternateName`, the country as an ISO code, the postal code in the shape its country's post uses (or leaves it out with a note), the telephone in international form from what was typed, hours in Schema.org's English day names and 24-hour times (a range such as `السبت-الخميس` walks the week forward, Saturday first), and coordinates as numbers. A test runs `local-business-invalid` and `address-schema-gulf` over what it writes.
- The Arabic plural of "tools" at 103 to 110 is «أدوات», not «أداة»: the hub test that matched the singular only now accepts both.

## 3. What the catalogue still lacks, and why (the real count is 108)

The catalogue promised 108 slugs. 77 are built now (`anchor-text` as `anchor-text-check`) and 31 are not; 31 of the 108 tools are not in the catalogue (the later batches' and the Arabic NLP tools). The 31, and why:

- **Needs the owner's Search Console connection** (Google's review of our OAuth application, issue #34): `traffic-at-risk`, `striking-distance`, `content-gap`, `consolidate`, `priorities`, `link-suggest`.
- **Needs a crawl of a whole site, or bulk requests to sites we do not own** (Pro features, phase 5; and an owner decision): `orphan-pages`, `link-graph`, `twin-diff`, `migration-check`, `duplicate-content-check`, `bulk-status`.
- **Heavy data processing the owner excluded, or an extension of the browser's report and the three-engine suites**: `log-analyzer`, `diacritics-clipping-check`, `rtl-ltr-compare`, `arabic-line-height-check`, `arabic-font-size-check` (the page's CSS is read only by a render), `font-weight-check` (what `arabic-font-slimmer` already says).
- **A new collector for information with no known effect**: `llms-txt-check`; **a new DNS or RDAP collector**: `dns-check`, `domain-check`; **no per-page rule to give an honest wrong and right example**: `keyword-cannibalization`, `schema-coverage`, `rich-results-check`, `content-quality`, `entity-consistency`, `cookie-consent-check`, `delivery-check` (the rule that exists needs the render).
- **Not useful beside what exists**: `robots-simulator` (= `robots-tester`), `machine-translation-signal`, `arabic-spelling-signals` (= `arabic-spell-check`).
