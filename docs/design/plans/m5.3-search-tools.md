# M5.3 Search-data tools and Google verification prep — Implementation Plan

> **ملخص للمالك:** الجزء الثالث من المرحلة 5 (مساعد الإصلاح ما زال مؤجَّلًا). ثلاث أدوات جديدة في فئة «بيانات البحث»: **«كلماتك في جوجل»** (تربط Search Console بلا حساب كما في التقرير، فتعرض أكثر كلمات موقعك بالعربية والنقرات والظهور ونسبة النقر والترتيب، و«الفرص القريبة» أي الكلمات في الصفحة الثانية (المركز 8 إلى 20)، وحصة الكلمات العربية من اللاتينية، والكلمات المكتوبة بإملاءين؛ لا يُحفظ شيء ويُلغى التصريح فور القراءة)، و**«اقتراحات البحث العربية»** (كلمة ← شجرة اقتراحات جوجل: الكلمة وأربع أدوات سؤال وحروف ا–ي، مرتَّبة بلا تكرار وتُصدَّر CSV)، و**«من ينافسك في جوجل»** (استعلامات حتى 5 ← نطاقات النتائج العشر الأولى، أيها موقع عربي، وهل ظهر نطاقك). الأخيرتان تسألان صفحات جوجل العامة وشروط جوجل لا تتيح ذلك، فكلتاهما **مطفأة** حتى تضع `ARABLYZER_SUGGEST=1` و`ARABLYZER_SERP=1`. ومعهما **تجهيز تحقق جوجل**: `docs/gsc-verification.md` (نص شاشة الموافقة، تبرير النطاق، سيناريو الفيديو، قائمة المتطلبات) وصفحات `/privacy` و`/terms` و`/security` بالعربية والإنجليزية. **المطلوب منك:** مراجعة النصوص العربية والقانونية (`reviewed: false`)، وقرار تشغيل العلمين، وتسليم طلب التحقق عند جاهزية النطاق (القسم 4).

**Goal:** Three tools that show a site's own search data (Search Console) and Google's public suggestions and results in Arabic, behind the guarantees the report's connection already has, and everything Google's OAuth verification asks of the site except the owner's own steps.

Design source: `google-signals.md` (the account-free connection: PKCE, an online token, nothing stored, a one-time result), `arabic-native.md` §12 (the suggest endpoint and its terms risk), `m5.2-integrations.md` (record style), R7/R8 design rules.

## 0. Measured before planning (2026-10-11)

| Question | Result |
|---|---|
| Can the report's connection start from a tool page? | Yes with two small changes: `start` takes `tool=keywords&site=<host>` where it took a report id, and the flow's `report` slot (cookie `r`, `Pending.report`) holds the word `keywords`; `callback` sends the visitor back to `/tools/gsc-keywords?gsc=<result id>` and `results/:id` is shared (read once, 5 minutes). Nothing else of the flow changes: PKCE, signed 10-minute cookie, online token, revoke in `finally`, one bucket. |
| What does Search Analytics give for page-two queries? | Rows sorted by clicks, each `clicks, impressions, ctr, position` for the 28 days ending two days ago. One `query` request of 1,000 rows is about 170 KB, under the 512 KB cap the Google client already enforces. A query that never got a click can fall below row 1,000: the page says when the cap was reached. |
| Where do the other two tools run? | In the API (not the scanner): it already has `safeFetch` with the egress policy (so the egress proxy in production), the limiter and Turnstile. The scanner's `ARABLYZER_SUGGEST` keeps serving the misspellings rule; the API reads the same variable for the tool, and compose passes it to both. |
| A tool without rules | `rules` is empty and `kind` is the new `search` (the tool's work is on its own routes, not a scan). `startScan` and the public API already refuse a tool whose kind is not `scan`; the page hides "the rules it runs", the completeness and example tests skip the empty set, and the API's tool list marks it `runnable: false` (it already does for `paste` and `generator`). |

## Global Constraints

- **Same guarantees as the report's connection** for «كلماتك في جوجل»: scope `webmasters.readonly` only; an online token, never a refresh token; the token goes in a header, never in a URL, log, error or result; revoked as soon as it was used; the result is read once and gone after five minutes; the typed site is held (with the PKCE verifier) for the 10 minutes of the flow and never logged. Without `ARABLYZER_GSC_CLIENT_ID/SECRET` the page shows a notice instead of the button.
- **The two Google-page tools are off by default** (`ARABLYZER_SUGGEST=1`, `ARABLYZER_SERP=1`, separate), and off means the route is a 404 and `status` says so. Both: one scan of the visitor's quota per request, taken like a scan (attempt throttle, Turnstile, the visitor's bucket, the network's) before Google is asked; one question to Google at a time with a pause; ArablyzerBot in the user agent; through `safeFetch`/the egress policy; answers kept a day in memory; the first answer that is not what we read ends the asking and is said on the page.
- **Suggestion tree:** a seed of 1 to 4 words (60 characters), 33 questions (the seed; كيف، لماذا، ما، أفضل before it; the 28 letters ا–ي after it), 0.5 s apart, 40 s at most, deduplicated by first appearance (seed, questions, letters), CSV built in the browser (a Blob, a UTF-8 BOM so Excel reads Arabic).
- **Results sampler: ToS risk, written down.** Google's terms do not offer its results page for automated use; it can change its markup, or block or ask for a check (consent page, CAPTCHA, "enable JavaScript"), which the tool recognises and reports as "Google asked for a check" instead of guessing. Mitigations: off by default; at most 5 queries a request (one scan unit); one every 2 s; stop at the first check; the organic results' **domains only** are kept (no address, title or snippet is kept or shown; the title is read only to decide whether the site is Arabic); a day's cache of lists we read; ArablyzerBot is not disguised. The owner decides whether to turn it on, knowing the results may not be readable at all from a server address.
- **Arabic-site detection (the tool's own, said on the page):** the result's title is mostly Arabic letters, or the domain ends in an Arab country's suffix (or is an Arabic IDN). A `.com` with an English title is not recognised.
- **Legal pages are factual and short**, written from what the product does (section 3), `reviewed: false` in Arabic and English; the owner and their counsel review them. Responsible-disclosure address `security@arablyzer.com`.
- UI on the R7 scale and the tool box, no `style=` (CSP), light and dark; AR+EN for every string; Latin digits in numbers (as the report's Search Console card); no new runtime dependency.

## 2. Contracts

**Keywords result** (`GscKeywordsResult`): `property` (or null), `period`, `totals`, `analysed`, `truncated`, `top` (50 by clicks), `opportunities` (positions 8 to 20, 5 impressions or more, by impressions, 50), `scripts` (queries, clicks, impressions for arabic, latin, mixed, other), `variants` (up to 10 groups of queries that fold to one spelling, hamza, ta marbuta, ya, marks and tatweel folded; the sum and up to 4 spellings), `partial`.

**`GET /api/search/status`** → `{suggest, serp}`. **`POST /api/search/suggest`** `{seed, turnstileToken}` → `{seed, groups[{kind: seed|question|letter, trigger, suggestions[]}], total, answered, asked, stopped: null|unreadable|time}`. **`POST /api/search/serp`** `{queries[1..5], domain?, turnstileToken}` → `{queries[{query, outcome: ok|blocked|unreadable|failed, results[{position, domain, arabic, signals[title|tld]}], you}], rivals[{domain, queries, best, arabic}], domain}`. Errors are a scan's (`bad-request`, `turnstile-failed`, `rate-limited` with `Retry-After`, `unavailable`); same-origin JSON only.

## 3. What the legal pages say (from what the product does)

- **Privacy:** a scan keeps the report (target, findings, measurements) under an unguessable address, for a stated time, deletable with the token given at the scan; nothing is kept of a visitor's address but a keyed hash in the limiter; no analytics or advertising cookies; Turnstile (Cloudflare) on the scan form; accounts (when on) keep the email, name and picture from Google sign-in, the saved sites, scans, alerts and keys, and the person can delete all of it; Search Console: the read-only scope, an online token used once and revoked, nothing of it stored; the typed site and the verifier held ten minutes; the results held five minutes and read once; the suggestion and results tools send the typed words to Google; the sub-processors (Cloudflare, Google), contact `privacy@arablyzer.com`.
- **Terms:** what the service is, acceptable use (scan sites you may scan, respect the limits, the bot's rules), the API keys, no warranty, liability limits, the open-source licence of the code, changes, contact. The jurisdiction and the company's legal name are placeholders the owner fills (`docs/gsc-verification.md`).
- **Security:** how to report (`security@arablyzer.com`, plain text or PGP if the owner adds a key), what is in scope, safe harbour in plain words, what the service does for its own safety (egress rules, no stored Google tokens, same-origin API, CSP), and `/.well-known/security.txt`.

## 6. As built (2026-10-11)

- **Where it differs from the plan above.** The keywords flow needed no change to the cookie or the stores: the word `keywords` takes the report id's place in the signed cookie and the pending record. The two Google-page tools live in the API, not the scanner, so each is a quota unit taken the way a scan is (attempt throttle, Turnstile, visitor's bucket, network's), with the flags read in `config.ts`. The tool page serves the island's form in the page as sent (the button waits for the API's word on the flag), because the site audit wants the tool before any section; the audit now knows the `search` kind (`FIELD_KINDS`) and that a tool with no rules lists only nearby tools in `#links`. `/.well-known/security.txt` is built with an `Expires` a year ahead (RFC 9116).
- **Numbers.** 3 tools (57 in all), 33 questions to the suggest endpoint (16 s of pauses, plus Google's answers), 5 queries and 2 s apart for the sampler, 1,000 queries read from Search Console. Tests: `gsc` 28 (8 new), `search` 22, the web model 12, legal 5; the site audit reads 440 pages with no problem.
- **Not done, on purpose.** The fix assistant (no Claude key). No live call to Google was made by a test or by the builder: the suggest and results parsers are checked against stand-in pages of the two markups Google is known to send, and the first real call is the owner's check (a "Google asked for a check" answer is the likely one from a server address). The legal pages carry no operator name or governing law.
- **Checked by eye.** Not in this record: the CI browser jobs open the three tool pages in all three engines; the layouts reuse the tool box, the cards and the report's Search Console card.
