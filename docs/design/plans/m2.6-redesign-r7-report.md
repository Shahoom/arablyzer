# M2.6 R7: the report page

## Record R7, report

The report page was rebuilt on 2026-10-03 on the branch `redesign/r7-report`, from `phase-2/redesign`
at `e7bca8c` (the record of R7a, the shared layer). It is the report's part of the owner's review of
the built v2 design («التصميم جدا سيء عالموبايل واظن كمان عالكمبيوتر»; the rules are in
[m2.6-redesign.md](m2.6-redesign.md) §8 and §8.1). What it touches: `components/ReportPage.astro`,
`islands/ReportApp.tsx`, `islands/report/*`, the report's words in `packages/i18n/src/report.ts`,
and, in `styles/global.css`, one rule removed and one added (a block at the foot headed
`/* R7 report */`). No shared piece (the header, the footer, the trail, `PageColumns`, `PageHead`,
`DocPage`, the scale and the tokens) is edited, and the data model, the states and the routing are
as they were.

What the owner saw, on a phone: the scanned address as a gradient pill that looks like a button,
mono pills for the date, the status and the rules' version, a lone logo square, a big stacked
summary, and a dock stuck to the foot of the page that covered the summary's heading. On a desktop:
one narrow column lost in 1440 px.

### The page

Every state is drawn by one frame (`islands/report/Frame.tsx`): a head, then the answer.

- **The head.** The address that was scanned, as a quiet bubble: `surface-2`, a hairline, read
  left to right whichever way the page runs, a globe before it and, when it is a link, a small
  arrow after it (mirrored in Arabic); no gradient, no shadow, no underline, so it cannot be
  taken for a button. Under it, one plain 13 px line: «فُحصت في 2026-10-03 · HTTP 200 · القواعد
  0.6.0» (and, for a tool's result, the tool first). Each fact is kept whole: the line breaks
  between them, never inside «HTTP 200». Then the page's heading, `heading-1` (28 px, 40 from lg),
  solid ink. There is no logo square.
- **Two columns from lg**, drawn by the island with `wrap`, `page-columns`, `page-main`,
  `page-aside` and `page-aside-sticky`, as `PageColumns.astro` draws an Astro page's. The aside is
  first in the page, so first on a phone, and sticks 24 px under the header from lg; the main
  column is at most 760 px, the aside 320 to 360 px, the content 1200 px. The aside is the summary
  (the score, the severities), the categories and the actions; the main column is what was found.
- **The finished report.**
  - The summary is a card with an 88 px ring (104 from lg) beside the severity pills and the
    sentence on the checks, and the link to the methodology. The ring's stroke follows the score's
    band, the cut-offs the category bars have had since R4 (90 and 50): the brand's gradient, amber
    to orange, the critical red. The number is solid, in the band's ink, and «من 100» is under the
    ring, not in it: «out of 100» does not fit inside 88 px.
  - Main: the line that says what was read (a quiet row as wide as its text, with the steps and
    the browsers behind it), the partial note and the notices, «ما وجدناه» with the severity filter
    (`chip`, one row that scrolls sideways on a phone if it must) and the findings as accordions
    (a card each, the most severe open; the message stops at 68 characters and the fix at 76),
    then the checks that did not fail, as the rows of one card, then the categories and the actions
    (on a phone), then the form that scans another page.
  - The categories (bars under 100, then the line of those at 100 and the line of those no rule
    applied to) and the actions (scan again, copy the link, JSON, and the line that says the report
    is not in search engines) are in the aside from lg. **On a phone they follow the checks**,
    which §8 asks («the aside's parts flow after the main content, except the report's summary»):
    each place has its own copy and the other is not displayed (`hidden lg:flex` and `flex
    lg:hidden`), so what is not shown is not read and not reached by the Tab key, and the reading
    order is the order on the screen at every width. A test holds it (below).
  - The form that scans another page is a heading and a plain `scan-box` after the report, not
    stuck to the foot of the screen. `html:has(.report-dock)` and its 9 rem of scroll padding are
    gone from `global.css`.
- **The scan under way.** The head (address, `heading-1`, the lead) over the same two columns: the
  aside is the scan box (a plain `scan-box` under `reading-beam`: the step it is on, «الخطوة 4 من
  6», a track of one piece for each step, and the queue's line when it is queued), the main
  column is the steps (a card, a timeline of 24 px discs, what each read in plain text), the
  browsers as chips, the log and the note. There is no turning ring (`scan-ring` is the home page's)
  and no dock: the box is the first thing on a phone, so the beam, the report's signature motion, is
  on the first screen, where a bar stuck to the foot of the page covered the steps. The beam
  crosses the box from the start of the line to its end and stops under reduced motion, as does the
  spinner and the pulse of the piece of the track under way.
- **The states that are one card** (failed, not found, blocked, opted out, offline): one centred
  column of at most 640 px, the address bubble above the card at its start edge, an icon tile of
  40 px, a `heading-1` heading, the words, the notices, the two buttons.

The words on the page are `text-body`, `text-small` and `text-meta`, the headings `heading-1`,
`heading-2` and `heading-3`, the controls `btn-grad`, `btn-white` and `chip`, the cards `card`,
`p-card` and `rounded-card`, never one in another (the folds are the rows of one card; the engines
inside the steps are flat tinted blocks), and the spacing the gutter's and the cards' steps. Only
weights 400 and 600, no `tracking-*`, and `start` and `end`, never `left` and `right`.

### Where this departs from §8

1. **The heading is above the summary card, not beside the ring.** §8 asks for «an 88 px ring beside
   the headline and the severity pills» on a phone. At `heading-1` the headline («مشكلتان وملاحظة
   واحدة في هذه الصفحة») is three lines in the 222 px that are left beside the ring, and at any
   smaller size the page's h1 would not be the size of every other h1 of the site (R7a's rule,
   which `heads.browser.test.ts` holds for the other pages). So the heading is the page's, in the
   head, and the card is the ring beside the severities and the sentence: a card of 200 px, not a
   column of ring, headline and pills (the stack the owner saw).
2. **The scan box is the aside, not a dock after the page.** §8 and R7a say the dock is not sticky
   and sits after the report. A dock after the page for the scan under way would be at the foot of
   a page of 1,600 px, off the first screen, and the beam would be seen by no one. The scan box is
   first on a phone and sticky beside the steps on a desktop; the form that scans another page is
   the dock that sits after the report.
3. **Categories and actions are drawn twice** (R7a says the whole aside comes first in the HTML on a
   phone; §8 says the aside's parts flow after the main content except the summary). Both are met:
   the summary is first in the HTML, the second half of the aside follows the checks on a phone,
   and each half has a copy for each place, one of them not displayed.
4. **The ring's stroke has bands** (a low score is amber or red, not always the brand's gradient),
   which §8 does not ask: a ring that is teal at 20 out of 100 says the wrong thing. Its number is
   solid, as asked.
5. **The skeleton of the report to come is gone** from the scan under way (a ring, a headline and two
   findings in quiet blocks, 190 px on a phone): it was decoration, and the page's two columns now
   stand where the report's will.
6. **Steps are plain.** The done steps are a solid teal disc, not the gradient's; what each read is
   plain text, not a pill; the track is a piece for each step, not a bar that fills between them.
7. **The footer waits while the report loads** (below). It is a rule in `global.css`, in the block
   the brief allows for report rules.
8. **The report is asked for while the scan is read** (below): a change in when a request is made,
   which a redesign does not usually carry, and which the page's score needed.

### What the review found

- **A layout shift of 0.33.** The island draws a blank placeholder until the API answers, and the
  footer under it was on the screen; when the report landed, the footer was thrown to the foot of
  it. Lighthouse scored the finished report 78 on a phone and 83 on a desktop, before and after the
  redesign alike (CI's set of pages leaves the report pages out, so nothing had measured it). The
  footer now waits, where it is but unseen (`visibility: hidden` while `[data-report-loading]` is
  on the page, `@media (scripting: enabled)` so that a visitor without scripts still has it), and
  is drawn with the first state. Layout shift 0.0001; the finished report is 95 on a phone and
  100 on a desktop.
- **Two round trips before the first line.** The report and the rules' fixes were asked for after
  the scan had been read, one after the other. They are asked for with it: the report, which is
  there already when the scan ended before the page opened (a shared link's is), and the fixes. What
  the early request found is trusted if it is the report or that there is none (the API answers 404
  for a scan it does not know and for one that failed); a report not stored yet (409) is asked for
  again. A page for a report that is not there asks once, where it asked twice. (This did not move
  Lighthouse's estimate: its phone figure is the chain of modules, the simulated 4× CPU and the
  fonts' 118 KB on a 1.6 Mbps link, not the order of two requests; it is the right order all the
  same.)
- **English phones score 97 in accessibility on every page** (`target-size`): the footer's links are
  23.5 px high in DM Sans (24 px is the minimum), 25.5 in Plex Arabic. It is R7a's footer, which this
  branch does not touch; `py-1` on its links, or `min-h-6`, is the fix.

  that is not found, with every accordion open; the keyboard on the accordions; the beam and the

  phone, inside its card, under 230 px; the address a quiet bubble (`surface-2`, no gradient, no
  shadow, no underline, read left to right, no logo square) and the line under it plain 13 px text
  on one line, no pill, no mono; the heading 28 px and 40 px, solid; the form after the report,
  not sticky, with no scroll padding for it, scrolling with the page; the Tab key's order on a

  Chromium and Firefox.
- **`report-model.test.ts`**: unchanged, the model is. `packages/i18n`: `thread.scan` (the bubble's
  verb) is gone, and the dock's `placeholder` is its `title`, now a visible heading.
- **Mutations tried** (each built and run, then undone): the aside not sticky, the second half of
  the aside shown on a phone too, `scan-ring` back on the scan box, a gradient on the address, the
  dock sticky again, the footer not held back, the heading at `heading-2`, the actions first on the
  screen and last in the page, the report asked for twice: each fails the test that holds it, in every
