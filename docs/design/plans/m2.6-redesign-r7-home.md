# Record R7 home: the home page after the owner's review (M2.6)

R7's home page was rebuilt on 2026-10-03 on the branch `redesign/r7-home`, from `phase-2/redesign` at `e7bca8c` (the tip after R7a).
The owner's review («التصميم جدا سيء عالموبايل واظن كمان عالكمبيوتر») found the v2 home page 10,620 px tall on a phone: a
heading of 40 px in three lines over a lead of four, a scan box of five rows, a wall of marquee chips under reduced motion,
five tall count cards, a desktop report squeezed into a phone, seven tall bento tiles, and plans, monitoring and questions as
long as a desktop's. This record says what the page is now, where it departs from §8, and what was run. It touches the home
page's components, its copy, the scan form's layout and the marquee's rules; the shared pieces (the header, the footer, the
trail, `PageColumns`, `PageHead`, `DocPage`, `heads.ts`, the scale and the tokens in `global.css`) are as R7a left them.
`global.css` got no `/* R7 home */` block: nothing was added to it, and the marquee's rules, which only this page used, moved
into the marquee's component.

## What changed

**The frame** (`Home.astro`, `SectionIntro.astro`). The sections under the hero sit in one column a `gap-section` apart (40,
56 and 72 px); the marquee and the counts, which are one strip of proof, 20 and 24 px apart. Every section's head is
`SectionIntro`: a kicker pill, an h2 of `heading-2` (22 px, 28 from lg; it was 30 and 44 by hand) and, for the bento and the
plans, a lead that a phone leaves out because it says what the section shows. `FinalCta` and `Monitoring` set their h2s the same
way. Each section's `contain-intrinsic-size` (the browser's placeholder for a section it has not laid out; 900 px for all of
them) is its measured height on a phone, a tablet and a desktop.

**The hero** (`Hero.astro`, `ScanForm.tsx`). The heading is 32 px on a phone, 44 on a tablet and 60 from lg (it was 40, 56 and
68), with its one gradient phrase. The lead is cut to two lines at 390 px, 16 px (18 from lg), in `ink` for the contrast on the
aurora: 99 characters in Arabic (168 before) and 89 in English (205), balanced. The badge is 32 px high (34 from md) with a 46 px
target made by its `::after`. The top padding, written for a two-row header, is 20 px (it was 48), 40 on a tablet and 56 on a
desktop. The aurora is at 70 % on a phone and fades out over its last 140 px, so that its clipped foot is not a line under the
product shot. The scan box keeps the turning ring and its glow (the only place they live). On a phone its field is a filled
48 px row, the button comes right after it (the DOM keeps the chips first, `order` moves the button, so the Tab order is the
field and the button), and what a scan runs is one line of 13 px text: «61 قاعدة» and the three browsers with their dots (the
label «كل الفحوص» is for sm and up, where the chips are as they were). The fine print under the button is untouched. The tool
chips are the shared `scroll-row` on a phone (one row, bleeding by the gutter, its end faded), a grid of three over three at md
and lg, and one centred row from xl: six chips do not fit one row of a tablet, and five and one is an orphan.

**The product shot** (`Showcase.astro`). On a phone and a tablet it is the short version: a window bar of 40 px with the
address pill, the score (a 96 px ring) beside the tally and the three severity pills, and the three findings as rows (title,
where, pill; their icons from md); 478 px in Arabic and 462 in English (it was about 1,120). The category bars, the overflow drawing and the floating
cards are a desktop's: the bars and the drawing from lg, the cards from xl. A phone's window is solid white (the glass and its
blur are from lg).

**The marquee** (`ToolsMarquee.astro`). One row of `chip`s (40 px on a phone, 36 from md) that slides with motion and, under
reduced motion, is the shared `scroll-row` (applied with `motion-reduce:scroll-row`): one row of the sixteen names, wider than
the screen, that scrolls sideways with an edge fade, on a phone bleeding to the screen's edges and from md staying one row (the
`scroll-row` wraps from md; here an override keeps it one row, faded at both ends). The chips are not links, so they do not lift
or take the pointer; the row is a stop of the Tab key (`role="group"`, `tabindex="0"`, named by its caption) that pauses the slide
and, under reduced motion, lets the arrow keys scroll it. Its rules (`marquee`, `marquee-track`, the keyframes) are the
component's, and are gone from `global.css`.

**The counts** (`Counts.astro`). A grid of two columns of tiles on a phone (64 px; 73 to 77 where a noun takes two lines), the number
(26 px, `gradient-text`) beside its noun, the longest noun last and across both columns; three tiles over two on a tablet; five
in a row from lg with the number (40 px) over its noun. The number's box is two digits wide, so counting up does not move its
noun. The strip is 229 px on a phone (it was 480).

**The bento** (`Bento.astro`, `BentoTile.astro`). On a phone: the first tile, the only one that shows real data, as a card with
its three browsers (the heading drawn in each, 24 px), and the six others as rows of an icon, a heading and a chevron (68 px), each
still the one link to its tool. The illustrations made of example data, and the «مثال» that marks them, are for md and up. From
md the tiles are as R2 drew them on the new scale (a 4-column grid from xl); the engines' rows are centred in their tall tile
and drawn larger (36 px) from xl. The section is 995 px on a phone (it was 3,219).

**How it works** (`HowItWorks.astro`). A column on a phone: each number (40 px) beside its title and words, a 2 px line
joining one to the next; three columns from md, centred, the numbers 48 px (they were 68), the reading beam on the line as before.

**Monitoring** (`Monitoring.astro`). The band's radius is 24 px (32 from md) and its padding 20 (40, 56). A phone leaves out
the four points, which say what the heading and the paragraph already say. The glows are two radial gradients on the panel
(`.band`), not two blurred elements: axe reads a blurred box as a solid colour and counts it behind any text whose box it
overlaps, even text outside the panel that the panel clips. It found the how-it-works step above the band on the teal glow
(4.2:1) and the live pill on both glows at once (3.7:1); a gradient is a background it sends to review, as it does for every other
gradient of the page, and the pixels are measured by the new contrast test (below).

**Plans** (`Plans.astro`). Three compact cards on a phone, in `card` and `p-card`; a paid plan's description, which says what its
points say, and the dashed «قريباً» foot, which repeats its tag, are for md and lg; the section's lead, which the note under the
cards repeats, is for md and up. There is still no price.

**The questions** (`Faq.astro`). Four cards, each summary a 56 px row (64 from md) with the card's own padding; the focus ring is
the card's, drawn outside it with its own radius (the first version clipped it: `overflow-hidden` on the card).

**The closing call** (`FinalCta.astro`). A band of 24 px radius (32 from md), `heading-2` in white, one line, one button.

## Where R7 home departs from §8

1. **The heading has a tablet size**, 44 px at md; §8 gives a phone's 32 and a desktop's 60.
2. **The counts' order changed** (the longest noun last, so that it spans the two columns of a phone), and a tablet has three tiles
   over two. The tiles are `card` and `p-card`, 64 px where a noun is one line and 73 to 77 where it is two (§8: 64 to 72).
3. **The product shot's rich layout is from lg, not md**: at 768 px the findings' column was 400 px wide and the evidence drawing
   was squeezed to a column of words, so a tablet has the phone's version, wider.
4. **Lines a phone leaves out because they repeat**: the bento's lead and the plans' lead (below md), the monitoring band's four
   points, a paid plan's description (below md) and its dashed foot (below lg). Nothing a phone cannot reach is left out of the
   page: the HTML has them, and what they say is said again in the heading, the paragraph and the tag beside them.
5. **The bento's illustrations are for md and up**, and the first tile keeps its content on a phone (§8: «a tighter grid or compact
   list»): the six rows are a list, the first tile a card, because it is the one that shows real data.
6. **The marquee's chips are not links** (R2 chose this: nothing moves under a keyboard) and use `chip` with the hover lift and the
   pointer taken off; the row is focusable so that a keyboard can pause it and, under reduced motion, scroll it. From md,
   reduced motion keeps one row (the shared `scroll-row` wraps from md), because sixteen names do not fit 1,200 px.
7. **The scan box's meta line is one line on a phone**, without its label «كل الفحوص», and the field has a fill there; the
   fine print is as it was.
8. **The monitoring band's glows are gradients**, for the reason above (axe), and the points of the band, hidden on a phone.
9. **The aurora is dimmer on a phone and fades out at the foot of the hero**, which §8 does not ask: the first screen of a
   phone was a wash of cyan, and the foot of the aurora was a line across the page.
10. **The hero's tool chips are three over three at md and lg**, and one row from xl (the English names need 990 px).

## Tests

- `test/home.test.ts` (15 tests, 2 new): the hero's lead stays under about a hundred characters in Arabic and ninety in English, and
  the badge is a count and a few words.
- `test/browser/home-r7.browser.test.ts` (new; 80 tests, 75 run in a Chromium-only-free engine set and 5 are Chromium's alone). In
  Chromium, Firefox and WebKit: the heading's size at 390, 768 and 1440 px and its one gradient phrase; the lead in two lines
  of 16 px; the scan box, its 48 px button and the chips on a phone's first screen; the chips as one scrolling row, three over
  three and one row; the badge's target; the form still saying what is wrong with an empty address; the marquee as one sliding
  row with motion and as one scrolling, faded row under reduced motion (390, 768 and 1440 px, both languages) and pausing under
  focus; the counts as a grid of two columns, three over two, five in a row, and counting up to the registry's numbers; the
  product shot's short version and its rich one; every section's heading on the scale, in solid ink and with no icon before it; no
  card inside a card; the bento as a card and six rows, and in full on a desktop; no illustration poking out of its box (the
  English «Arabic digits rejected» did); every control 40 px or more on a phone; the questions as 56 px rows with a focus ring
  that the card does not clip; the phone page under 7,000 px in both languages; no sideways scroll at 320 to 1440 px, both
  languages, with and without motion; no animation under reduced motion; no style attribute or inline handler. And in Chromium,
  the contrast of every line of the whole page, from its pixels, at 390 and 1440 px in both languages (the dark band and the
  closing band are below the first screen that `contrast.browser.test.ts` measures).
- `eslint.config.js`: a block of its own lets the new test import Playwright (not the shared list, which three other branches edit).
- **Mutations tried** (each built and run, then undone): the heading at 40 px on a phone (3 failures, one in each engine), the
  marquee without `scroll-row` under reduced motion (3), the counts in one column (3), the bento's rows showing their illustration
  (3), a section heading hand-set at 30 px (3), a card inside a card in the plans (3), the category bars on a phone (3), the typed
  name's caret kept under reduced motion (2: the contrast test), the long English pill (3), the questions' card clipping its
  content (2: Chromium and Firefox), a page grown past 7,000 px (3), the lead back at its long copy (3). The first mutation of
  the counts was **missed**: the test counted the columns by the distinct left edges of the tiles, and in a one-column grid
  the tile that spans two columns makes an implicit one and starts a gap to the side. It counts the tiles of the first row now.
