# M2.6: the v2 redesign (a lively SaaS look)

## 1. Decision

On 2026-10-02 the owner rejected the calm first redesign («مافيه حياة ابدًا ومو كانه saas»), then
adopted the v2 design («اعتمد هالتصميم حاليًا») and asked for it to be built now («اعمل التصميم
الجديد هلا»). This milestone replaces the Lab look of the public site with v2. The dashboard is
Phase 4 and is not built here.

The visual specification is the Design canvas the owner approved:
<https://claude.ai/artifact/Hd938Uf252uncg1qUcgQGJ>, row 1 (Main, DashSite, DashHome). The
source of each artboard is kept in `docs/design/v2/`: `Main.dc.html` (home), `DashSite.dc.html`
and `DashHome.dc.html` (dashboard, for buttons, chips, cards and charts). They are the canvas's
own format: HTML with `{{holes}}` filled by the class at the end of each file. Row 2 of the canvas (scan, report, tool page,
knowledge, mobile) is still v1 in layout: build those screens with v1's structure and v2's
tokens and components.

Type: IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic and DM Sans for Latin, in one stack (`"DM Sans", "IBM Plex
Sans Arabic"`), both self-hosted from Fontsource through Astro's fonts API; IBM Plex Mono stays
for code. Weights 400, 500, 600 and 700. Arabic is never letter-spaced.

Radius by role: the scan box 24 px, tiles 24 px, cards 16–20 px, buttons 12–14 px, chips 999 px.
One soft shadow scale (`shadow`, `shadow-lg`). The category colours stay as tokens, one per
category, for chips, dots and tile icons.

Motion, all stopped by `prefers-reduced-motion`: the aurora behind the hero (slow drift), the
rotating gradient ring of the scan box, the floating cards of the showcase, the tools marquee,
the count-up of the numbers, the letters of «متجر العطور» parting in WebKit's row, the typed name
in the forms tile, and the reading beam while a scan runs (right to left, the way Arabic reads).

## 3. Milestones

- **R1, foundation.** The tokens and utilities above in `apps/web/src/styles/global.css`, the
  fonts, the header (sticky, translucent, the sections, the language link, the scan button), the
  footer (columns), the logo (the gradient mark), the theme colour, and buttons, chips, pills and
  cards as components. Every existing page keeps building and passing its tests.
- **R2, home.** Hero (aurora, headline with the gradient phrase, lead, the scan box with the
  ScanForm island inside, tool chips that link to tool pages, the trust line), the showcase
  (golden page 04 from `lib/showcase.ts`), the tools marquee (from the registry), the counts
  (from the registries), the bento tiles, how it works, the monitoring band (marked «قريباً»),
  plans (free now; paid plans «قريباً», with no prices until the owner sets them), the FAQ and the
  final call to scan.
- **R3, tool pages and the tools index.** The scan box with the tool's pill, then what it checks,
  the example, the fix, the FAQ as accordions and related tools as cards.
- **R4, report and scan progress.** The report as a thread under the scanned address: score ring,
  counts, categories, findings as accordions with their evidence; the reading beam on the scan
  box while the scan runs.
- **R5, knowledge.** A hub that searches rules, fix guides, glossary terms and tools in one box,
  and the restyled pages under it, the bot page, the methodology page and 404.
- **R6, checks.** Both languages; the self-scan, the SEO package's site audit, the browser
  tests and the Lighthouse budgets; screenshots of each page at 390 px and 1440 px.

## 4. Rules that bind every milestone

- The Content-Security-Policy hashes inline `<style>` and `<script>` only: no `style="…"`
  attributes in Astro markup. CSS lives in the stylesheet (Tailwind utilities or `@layer
  components`); islands may set styles from script.
- No horizontal overflow at 390 px: the aurora and the marquee are clipped by their section.
- Text contrast 4.5:1 (3:1 from 24 px bold); gradient text only on large bold type, with the
  darker stops above. Focus is visible on every control.
- No invented numbers: counts come from the registries, the showcase from the golden report,
  anything else is marked «مثال». No prices until the owner decides them.
- New words go in `packages/i18n` in Arabic and English, `reviewed: false`.
- The site must pass its own rules (the self-scan): this redesign is judged by Arablyzer itself.
