# M2.6: the v2 redesign (a lively SaaS look)

## 1. Decision

On 2026-10-02 the owner rejected the calm first redesign («مافيه حياة ابدًا ومو كانه saas»), then
adopted the v2 design («اعتمد هالتصميم حاليًا») and asked for it to be built now («اعمل التصميم
الجديد هلا»). This milestone replaces the Lab look of the public site with v2. The dashboard is
Phase 4 and is not built here.

The visual specification is the Design canvas the owner approved:
<https://claude.ai/artifact/Hd938Uf252uncg1qUcgQGJ>, row 1 (Main, DashSite, DashHome). The
source of each artboard is kept in `docs/design/v2/`: `Main.dc.html` (home), `DashSite.dc.html`
and `DashHome.dc.html` (dashboard, for buttons, chips, cards and charts). They are the canvas's
own format: HTML with `{{holes}}` filled by the class at the end of each file. Row 2 of the canvas (scan, report, tool page,
knowledge, mobile) is still v1 in layout: build those screens with v1's structure and v2's
tokens and components.

## 3. Milestones

- **R1, foundation.** The tokens and utilities above in `apps/web/src/styles/global.css`, the

  cards as components. Every existing page keeps building and passing its tests.
- **R2, home.** Hero (aurora, headline with the gradient phrase, lead, the scan box with the
  ScanForm island inside, tool chips that link to tool pages, the trust line), the showcase
  (golden page 04 from `lib/showcase.ts`), the tools marquee (from the registry), the counts

  plans (free now; paid plans «قريباً», with no prices until the owner sets them), the FAQ and the

- **R3, tool pages and the tools index.** The scan box with the tool's pill, then what it checks,
  the example, the fix, the FAQ as accordions and related tools as cards.
- **R4, report and scan progress.** The report as a thread under the scanned address: score ring,
  counts, categories, findings as accordions with their evidence; the reading beam on the scan
  box while the scan runs.
- **R5, knowledge.** A hub that searches rules, fix guides, glossary terms and tools in one box,
  and the restyled pages under it, the bot page, the methodology page and 404.
- **R6, checks.** Both languages; the self-scan, the SEO package's site audit, the browser
  tests and the Lighthouse budgets; screenshots of each page at 390 px and 1440 px.

## 4. Rules that bind every milestone

- The Content-Security-Policy hashes inline `<style>` and `<script>` only: no `style="…"`
  attributes in Astro markup. CSS lives in the stylesheet (Tailwind utilities or `@layer
  components`); islands may set styles from script.
- No horizontal overflow at 390 px: the aurora and the marquee are clipped by their section.
- Text contrast 4.5:1 (3:1 from 24 px bold); gradient text only on large bold type, with the
  darker stops above. Focus is visible on every control.
- No invented numbers: counts come from the registries, the showcase from the golden report,
  anything else is marked «مثال». No prices until the owner decides them.
- New words go in `packages/i18n` in Arabic and English, `reviewed: false`.
- The site must pass its own rules (the self-scan): this redesign is judged by Arablyzer itself.

## 5. Record R1

R1, the foundation, was built on 2026-10-02 on the branch `phase-2/redesign`. No page body was
redesigned: each keeps its Lab layout, in v2 colours and type, until its own milestone.

### What changed

- **Tokens** (`apps/web/src/styles/global.css`). The table of §2 is the `@theme`: every colour, the
  gradients, one radius scale (`xs` 6 px to `3xl` 32 px, by role), the two soft shadows (`shadow`,
  `shadow-lg`) and the glow of a gradient button. `--color-*: initial` stays, so only these colours
  exist. They are CSS variables on `:root`, so a dark theme is a matter of redeclaring them. The
  body is the v2 ground; the grid paper is gone. The base layer sets `line-height: 1.7`, a focus
  ring (`--focus-ring`: indigo, and a lighter one on the dark panels), `scroll-padding-top` for the
  sticky header, and `--reading` and `--grad-dir`, which turn what runs toward the end of the line
  (the beam, the marquee, the headline gradient) with the page's direction.
- **Components, as `@utility`**, so they take `md:` and `hover:` and are left out of a page that
  does not use them: `btn-grad`, `btn-white`, `btn-ghost`, `btn-light`, `btn-outline-light`,
  `btn-lg`; `chip` (with `aria-pressed`); `kick`; `sev` with `sev-critical`, `-serious`,
  `-moderate`, `-minor`, `-info`, `-pass`; `card`, `card-lift`; `wrap` (the 1240 px page width);
  `gradient-text`; `logo-mark`; `panel-dark`; and the motion: `aurora`, `aurora-blob`,
  `aurora-grid`, `scan-ring`, `scan-glow`, `reading-beam`, `marquee`, `marquee-track`. Each
  animation has its own `prefers-reduced-motion` rule: the aurora and the ring stand still, the
  beam is not drawn, and the marquee becomes a wrapped row (its aria-hidden copy dropped); read in
  Chromium from the computed `animation-name` of each, with the preference on and off. In forced
  colours a button keeps an edge, a pressed chip a heavier one, and the gradient text falls back to
  the system colour. A standalone page with every utility was drawn right to left in Chromium,
  Firefox and WebKit, and left to right in Chromium, and measured at 390 px in both directions:
  nothing scrolls sideways.
- **Type.** `@fontsource/dm-sans` 5.3.0, self-hosted, weights 400 to 700; IBM Plex Sans Arabic
  now in 400, 500, 600 and 700. The stack is `DM Sans, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, …` (`--font-sans`).

### Performance: the weights a page asks for

CI's Lighthouse gate is 95 on a phone, and the first run of R1 was under it: the home page fell
from 99 to 95 and a tool page from 98 to 94, because the first layout asks for every weight in use,
and each Plex Arabic file is 45 KB; the simulated first contentful paint went from 1.35 s to 2.1 s.
So the chrome uses the two weights a page already loads: links regular (the current section
semibold), headings semibold, and `b`, `strong` and `th` semibold in the base layer (with a 700
face declared, a `<strong>` in the methodology page had started to fetch it). The wordmark stays DM
Sans 700 (14 KB). No page of the 334 asks for Plex Arabic 500 or 700, or DM Sans 500, on first load
(a phone viewport, counted in Chromium). Measured with the CI's script on twelve pages (both
languages' home, tools index and tool page, the rule library and a rule, the fix index, the
glossary, the methodology, the bot page), one run each: mobile 97 to 99 (before R1: 97 to 99),
desktop 100, accessibility, best practices and SEO 100 on every one, layout shift at most 0.008.

### Where R1 departs from §2

1. `field` is #8491a3, not #8a97a8: the plan's value measures 2.97:1 on white and 2.79:1 on the
   ground, short of the 3:1 an input's edge needs; this one is 3.20:1 and 3.01:1.
2. DM Sans has no fallback, and IBM Plex Sans Arabic no Latin subset. Astro's metric-matched
   fallback is a face of a local Arial with no unicode-range; between DM Sans and Plex it would draw
   the Arabic letters itself, and Arablyzer's own `ar-font-no-arabic` would be right to fail the
   site. And a Latin face of Plex would be fetched in the moments before DM Sans arrives.
3. The header bar is 80 % opaque, not 72 %: over a dark panel scrolling beneath it, its text stays
   above 4.5:1.
4. `html` has `line-height: 1.7`, which the artboard sets on its root.
5. The chrome's weights (above): the artboards' 500 for links and 700 for headings cost the
   phone score the CI gates.
