# M5.4 Daily monitoring — Implementation Plan

> **ملخص للمالك:** المراقبة اليومية إعداد في الخطة لا ميزة منفصلة: `ARABLYZER_PLAN_<PLAN>_MONITOR_EVERY_DAYS=1`. الخطة المجانية تبقى أسبوعية كما قررت (الافتراضي 7)، واليومية لا تُبلَغ إلا حين توجد خطة مدفوعة تقرأ هذا الإعداد (M4.4: لا أحد يُحلّ إلى خطة مدفوعة بعد). المجدول صار يقرأ فاصل الخطة عند كل تشغيل في الاتجاهين. لا جدول جديد ولا هجرة ولا متغير جديد. **ينتظرك:** لا شيء الآن؛ عند بناء الخطط المدفوعة تضع `…_PRO_MONITOR_EVERY_DAYS=1`.

**Goal:** A plan decides how often a monitored site is scanned. The free account stays weekly; a paid plan can be daily; nothing else changes.

Built on `m4.3-monitoring.md` (the `monitorEveryDays` plan field, the scheduler, `monitors.every_days`).

## 0. What was already there, and what was not

| Piece | State before |
|---|---|
| `AccountPlan.monitorEveryDays`, `ARABLYZER_PLAN_<PLAN>_MONITOR_EVERY_DAYS` (whole number ≥ 1) | there; a paid plan read it too, but started from the *development* plan, not the free account's |
| The scheduler's interval | `max(stored, plan)`: a monitor enabled while weekly **stayed weekly** after the plan became daily; only a downgrade took effect |
| The API's `everyDays` for a monitor | the stored value, which can lag the plan |
| English copy for one day | "every 1 day" |

## Changes

1. `packages/plans`: a paid plan's monitoring starts from the configured free account's numbers (so setting `MONITOR_EVERY_DAYS=1` is all a daily plan needs), and `planCatalogFrom` refuses to start when a paid plan watches **fewer sites or less often** than the free account (same spirit as the anonymous-visitor rule).
2. `apps/api/src/monitor/scheduler.ts`: the interval of a run is the plan's `monitorEveryDays`, in both directions. `monitors.every_days` is written at each run as before (the admin panel shows it).
3. `apps/api/src/monitor/schedule.ts`, `monitors.ts`, `sites.ts`: the page reads the plan's interval (`everyDays`), not the possibly stale stored one.
4. `packages/i18n/src/account.ts`: "Scanned automatically every day" (the Arabic form already said «كل يوم»).
5. Docs: `infra/.env.example`, `infra/README.md`, `docs/deploy/staging.md`.

## Tests

- `packages/plans`: a paid plan can be daily while the account is weekly; it inherits the account's numbers when it sets none; it is refused when it watches fewer sites or less often.
- `apps/api` scheduler: a monitor enabled weekly runs daily once the plan is daily; a daily monitor starts exactly one scan a day over three days of 15-minute ticks (no double slot); the existing downgrade test still raises a short interval to the plan's.
- `apps/api` routes: a plan of one day shows `everyDays: 1` on the toggle's answer and on `GET /api/sites`.

## Not in M5.4

- **A shorter interval takes effect at the monitor's next run**, not at once: the next date was set under the old interval. When M4.4 moves a person to a paid plan it should pull the date forward (a store method for it, called where the plan changes); until a person can be on a paid plan there is nothing to call it from. A person can also turn monitoring off and on to be scanned within minutes.
- Choosing an interval per site, a per-account override of the interval in the owner's panel (the owner decided the free plan stays weekly), and anything below one day.
- The paid plans themselves, prices and payments.

## For the owner

Nothing now. When paid plans are built: `ARABLYZER_PLAN_PRO_MONITOR_EVERY_DAYS=1` (and `…_MONITORED_SITES`) next to the paid plan's five required numbers. Daily monitoring multiplies the scans the monitor starts by seven for each site, inside the queue's lower half and the per-host bucket as before; the plan's `HISTORY_DAYS` should keep at least two days so the previous scan is there to compare.
