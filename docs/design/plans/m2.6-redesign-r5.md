# M2.6 R5: the knowledge hub and the content pages

## Record R5

R5 was built on 2026-10-02 on the branch `redesign/r5-knowledge`, from `phase-2/redesign` (R1, the
foundation, is its base). It adds the knowledge hub, one search over every tool, rule, fix guide and
glossary term, and gives the pages under it, the bot page, the methodology and the 404 page the v2
look. R2 (home), R3 (tool pages) and R4 (report) are built beside it on their own branches; R5
touches none of their files, except where §"Where this branch meets the others" says.

### The knowledge hub

`/knowledge` and `/en/knowledge`, in `PATHS` as `knowledge`, in the `pages` sitemap, in the header's
`NAV` (second, after the tools) and so in the footer, which takes `NAV` from there. Its Open Graph
card is drawn with the section's name as its kicker (`scripts/og-images.ts`). Meta, canonical, the
hreflang pair with x-default, and JSON-LD of a `BreadcrumbList` and an `ItemList` of the four
directories, as the other index pages have them.

- **Structure** (the approved canvas's v1 artboard «المعرفة», in v2): the head, with the aurora of the
  home page's hero behind it, a gradient «المعرفة» and its lead; one big search box; the chips
  (الكل، الأدوات، القواعد، إصلاح Search Console، المصطلحات) with live counts; the results grouped by
  kind as linked rows (title, a line of text, a rule's severity pill, the category in a pill with
  its dot); an empty state; and four tiles with the registries' own totals, which lead to the
  directories. A group's heading links to its directory.
- **The index** (`lib/knowledge.ts`, at build time): every tool, rule, fix guide and glossary term
  in the page's language, each with the other language's title, its id or slug and its category, in
  the directories' own order. Totals are the registries': 44 tools, 61 rules, 15 guides and 38
  terms today, 158 rows; a unit test holds the index to the registries in both languages. A tile's
  noun takes the form Arabic gives its number («15 دليلاً»), from `arabicCount`.
- **The search** (`islands/KnowledgeSearch.tsx`, Preact, `client:load`, 12.4 KB and 4.7 KB gzipped
  with its helpers): the tools' search's reading of Arabic (`lib/search.ts`: hamza forms, taa
  marbuta, alef maqsura, tatweel, harakat, the article), over the title in both languages, the id,
  the line of text, the category and a rule's severity; every word must be found; those whose
  names have every word come before those that only mention it (`robots` finds the checker before
  the rules). The pure part (`lib/knowledge-search.ts`) is tested without a browser.
- **Without script.** The island renders the groups on the server, so the HTML holds all 158 pages
  as plain links and the chips; the script only narrows them. Words typed before the script has
  loaded are kept (the field is not controlled; a browser test holds the script back and types);
  `?q=` and `?type=` fill the search and what is typed goes back into the address, so a search can
  be linked to; the 404 page uses that.
- **Weight.** The hub is 389 KB of HTML in Arabic (57.9 KB gzipped) and 355 KB in English
  (52.4 KB), against 158 KB (26 KB) for the rule library: the island's props repeat the lists' text
  once (99 KB of the 389), and each row carries its classes. The first group is rendered at once
  and the others by `content-visibility: auto`, which keeps the first paint cheap. A phone scores
  97 and 98 in Lighthouse (the rule library, 99); a smaller page is possible (§Left).
- **The toolbar** (search and chips) stays under the header from 1024 px wide and 760 px tall, as a
  glass card; below that, where it would take a third of the screen, it scrolls away.

### Why the hub's rows hold a rule's quote of its example as code

The first build of the hub failed the site's own audit: the rows show each rule's first reason, and
the reasons of `ar-tatweel` and `form-arabic-digits-rejected` quote what they judge, «مـحـمـد» and
«١٢٣», which `ar-tatweel` and `ar-digits-mixed` then found on the hub (and the digits against the
Western ones of its counts). In the rules' copy those quotes are code (`` `مـحـمـد` ``), which the
rules do not read. `description` is plain text, for the meta description, so `scripts/rule-data.ts`
writes a second field, `summary`: the same first point with its backticks, cut where the
description is, so that without them it is the description (a test holds that for every rule and
language). The island sets the code parts in `<code dir="ltr">`.

### The content pages

- **Shared**: `PageHead` (the head of a directory: trail, kicker, heading, lead, over the tint of
  `page-wash`), `Breadcrumb` (home, steps, this page; Bidi-set), `DocHeading` (a document section's
  heading with its number in the indigo badge). `DocPage` is the layout of every document: the head
  over the tint, the sections beside an aside that stays in view on a wide screen, which comes after
  the sections on a phone (links to more) or before them (`asideFirst`: a page's contents).
  `LinkBox` and `Contents` are cards; the questions of a guide or a term are each a card with the
  answer showing.
- **Rule library and rule pages**: the head with the search (the tools' reading of Arabic, not a
  lower-case match), the categories in a card that stays in view, the rules as cards. A rule's
  page: the trail, its id, severity and facts as chips, the sections numbered, the example in two
  cards with a pill each, how it is detected as numbered steps in the gradient of the home page's,
  its weight as the gradient number of a card. Anchors (`#why`, `#example`, `#fix`, `#detect`,
  `#references`, `#cat-…`), headings, JSON-LD and the order the audit reads are as they were.
- **Fix guides and glossary**: the two indexes as cards (the guides in Search Console's two
  groups, dotted in the severities' colours), a link to the hub in each head; a guide and a term
  under `DocPage`.
- **Bot page, methodology, 404**: the document layout with numbered contents; the bot's code blocks
  are dark, scroll inside themselves and take the keyboard. The 404 is a gradient «404» over the
  tint, the four ways on as cards, and a search that sends the visitor's words to the hub.
- **The bot page's overflow at 360 px** (it had 8 px, since before R1): `Rich` set every code chip
  `nowrap`, and the DoH resolver's address, 36 characters, is wider than the line. Code longer
  than 32 characters (`WHOLE`, the number the rules' pages already use) now breaks where it must;
  shorter code stays whole. A browser test holds every page of R5 to no overflow at 360 and 390 px,
  in both languages and in the three engines; with the old `Rich` it fails with «expected 368 to be
  less than or equal to 360», in all three.

### A heading that moves when DM Sans arrives (found by Lighthouse's desktop run)

CI's desktop gate is 100, and the first full Lighthouse run of R5's pages failed it on one page:
`/glossary/ai-crawlers` scored 99, with 0.073 to 0.079 of layout shift in all three runs. Every
Arabic page preloads Plex Arabic's two weights and not DM Sans, which draws its Latin letters (a
term in a heading, the names of crawlers in a lead); the text is drawn at once in the system's
letters, DM Sans arrives after the first paint, the heading or the lead wraps differently, and the
page under it moves. It comes and goes with what Chrome has cached from the page before it. It is
not new: the base commit (R1) shows 0.103 on the same page in the same sequence. Holding every
font that is not preloaded back for 600 ms, as a slow network does, gave 0.16 on a fix guide in R5's
layout (0.002 before) and 0.04 to 0.07 on the terms.

`Page.astro` takes `latinPreload` (default false, so no page of R2, R3 or R4 changes) and every page
of R5 sets it, which preloads DM Sans' 400 and 600 as English pages already do. The same Lighthouse
run, repeated: layout shift 0 to 0.0007 in all 84 runs, and the phone's first contentful paint of
the Arabic pages 1.20 s where it was 1.35 s. Two browser tests hold it (four font preloads; and, in
Chromium, under 0.01 of layout shift with the other fonts held back, which is 0.16 and 0.04
without). The same fix would serve every Arabic page of the site; that is left to the owner of the
fonts (R6), with the numbers above.

### CSS, and the weights

`global.css` gains, under `/* R5 */` at its end: `chev-end` (a chevron toward the end of the line,
drawn with two borders, for lists of hundreds of links), `page-wash` (the tint behind a head: two
soft lights, the aurora without its motion, its blur or its grid) and `doc-links` (links in a
document's prose are `brand-ink`). Nothing else is shared. Only 400 and 600 are used: `chip` is 500
in R1, so the hub's chips set `font-normal!`, and no page of R5 loads Plex Arabic 500 or 700, or DM
Sans 500 (read from `document.fonts` on twelve pages of R5, a phone's viewport). No `style`
attribute: all 336 built pages have none, and each of their 1,438 inline `<style>` and 192 inline
`<script>` is in its page's CSP (checked on the build).

### Copy

`packages/i18n/src/knowledge.ts` (new, in `ALL_COPY`, `reviewed: false`): the hub's words, Arabic
and English, with the plural forms of both. `site.ts`: `nav.knowledge` («المعرفة» / "Knowledge"), and
the footer's library column, whose first link is now the hub, is headed «تعلّم» / "Learn", so it
does not open with its own name. `./copy` is exported, for `codeParts` in the island. The owner's

### Where R5 departs from the canvas and the plan

1. **Rows show a line of text for every kind.** The v1 artboard gives rules and guides a title and
   pills only; a rule's `description` and a guide's were written for cards, and a search that
   finds a row by its text should show it. Clamped to two lines.
2. **The chips wrap** on a phone instead of scrolling: the last two would have been out of sight.
3. **A guide's tag is Search Console's group** («صفحات لم تتم فهرستها»), not the constant
   «Search Console» the artboard shows, which would be the same on all fifteen.
4. **The lead of the hub is ink, not ink-2.** On a phone the aurora's blobs sit behind it: its
   worst point measured 4.1:1 in Arabic and 3.7:1 in English. Axe cannot read a gradient behind
   text, so the self-scan only asks for a review there; the numbers come from drawing each page with
   its text invisible and comparing the pixels under every box with the text's colour. With ink,
   every text on the hub's first screen, at 360, 390, 768 and 1440 px, is at least 5.2:1 (the
   breadcrumb's «الرئيسية» is the tightest) and the lead is above 7.7:1. The same probe, on twenty
   pages of R5 (both languages) at 360, 390 and 1440 px, found no text under 4.5:1.
5. **The footer's heading** (above).
6. **The artboard's own header and footer** (its «دخول» and «الأسعار», v1 chrome) are not drawn: the
   hub sits in R1's header and footer.

### Left

- **The hub's weight.** 389 KB is more than the others, and mobile performance is 97 for it, not
  99. The island's props could come from a script the page loads (the data once, in a cacheable
  file) and the rows' classes could be a component class: perhaps 150 KB less. Not needed for the
  gate.
- **`latinPreload` for every Arabic page** (above), and `chip`'s weight (500 in `global.css`, set
  back by each page that uses it): R6's, with the rest of the fonts.
- **Tools index and tool pages** (R3) still have the Lab look and no link to the hub; the hub lists
  them and links to their pages.
- The copy is `reviewed: false`, like the rest: the owner reads `knowledge.ts`, the nav labels
  and the footer's «تعلّم» before CI's review job goes green.
- A dark theme is not started; every colour R5 added is a token.
