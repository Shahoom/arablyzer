# M2.6 R7: the knowledge hub and the content pages

## Record R7 (knowledge)

R7 (knowledge) was built on 2026-10-03 on the branch `redesign/r7-knowledge`, from `phase-2/redesign` at
`e7bca8c` (the record of R7a). It rebuilds the knowledge hub and the content pages for the owner's review
(§8 of [m2.6-redesign.md](m2.6-redesign.md)): the hub, the rule library and a rule, the fix guides and a
guide, the glossary and a term, the bot's page, the methodology and the 404 page, in Arabic and English.
Three other branches rebuild the home page, the tools and the report beside it; this one stays inside its
files (listed at the end) and changes no shared piece but one test (below).

What the owner saw in the R6 build, and what it was in these pages: the hub was 24,147 px tall on a phone
(158 rows of 100 to 150 px each, a filter after them, two icon squares and a gradient number on every tile);
the documents were one narrow column in the middle of 1440 px; paragraphs, headings and numbers were set by
hand (17 px paragraphs, a 56 px gradient score, a 168 px gradient «404», indigo and grey numbers beside the
headings, a card for every question).

## What changed

### The hub (`/knowledge`)

- **Two columns** (`KnowledgeSearch.tsx` draws its own `page-columns`, `PageHead` is the head). The main
  column holds the search and the results; the aside, sticky from lg, is the type filter as a vertical list
  with its counts. **Below lg the same filter is a `scroll-row` of chips above the results** (a filter after
  158 rows was out of reach): two renderings of one state, each hidden where it is not wanted, so a screen
  reader meets one group. `?q=` and `?type=` still fill the search; the words typed before the script loads
  are kept (the field is not controlled).
- **Compact rows** in one card for each group (`kb-list`, `kb-row`): a dot in the colour of the row's
  category (a quiet one for a term), the title at 16 px, the summary at 14 px (one line on a phone, two from
  md), a rule's severity pill, and from md the category as quiet text and a chevron. A group shows its first
  **five rows** (`GROUP_LIMIT`); the rest are behind **a native «show all»** (`<details>`), so it works without
  script and every page is still a link in the HTML (the test of R5 that reads all 158 links before and after
  the script still holds). A group that is only a row or two over the limit is shown whole. One kind chosen
  (a chip, a row of the list) shows all of it; a search with few matches shows them whole.
- The group headings are `heading-2` with the count as quiet meta and the link to the directory at the end
  (44 px high on a phone); the tiles at the foot lose their icon squares and gradient numbers (a solid
  `heading-2` number and a line of ink-2).
- The island is 11.6 KB (12.0 before). The hub's HTML is 379 KB (410 before); `content-visibility` is gone,
  since a closed disclosure is not laid out.

### The rule page

`RulePage` is on `PageColumns` as `DocPage` is: its own `lg:grid-cols-[…]` and `lg:sticky lg:top-24` are
gone. The head keeps the trail, the h1, the rule's id (a dark code chip) and its severity pill; the version,
what it reads, «needs review» and the WCAG criteria are **one quiet line** where there were five outlined
chips (the category is the trail's last step). The aside holds the contents (from lg), the weight in the
score (a **solid number in `heading-2`**, beside its title: it was a 56 px gradient), and the pages the rule
points to as one card: the tools that run it and the full scan, the rules near it, **the fix guides and the
glossary terms that name it** (34 of the 61 rules have terms, 3 have guides: the guides and terms already
pointed at the rules, and now the rule points back). The numbers of «how we detect» are a quiet tint, not
the home page's gradient.

### The documents (rule, fix guide, term, bot, methodology)

- Paragraphs, lists and the FAQ are `text-body` (16 px, Arabic's leading), not `text-base leading-[1.85]
  md:text-[17px]`; the h3s are `heading-3`.
- **No numbers**: `DocHeading` is the `h2` alone, and `Contents` lists the sections without its indigo
  mono numbers, so the two cannot disagree (§8: «one look, or drop both»).
- **Contents in two forms** (`Contents.astro`): from lg a card in the sticky aside; below lg a shut
  disclosure under the head (48 px), whose links are 44 px. The aside comes after the text on a phone for
  all five pages (the bot's page and the methodology put it first in R7a).
- **The pages a document points to are one card** (`Related.astro`), a group of links for each kind, where a
  guide had up to four boxes; the bot's page and the methodology have one too (the methodology, the rules,
  the tools; the rules, the bot, the tools). The id line of a related rule is not repeated in the aside.
- The questions of a guide or a term are a list with a hairline between them, not a card each; the head's
  «other language» and «updated» lines are one line of meta.

### The directories

`RulesIndex`, `FixIndex` and `GlossaryIndex` are `PageHead`, then `PageColumns`: a main column of compact rows
(one card for each group, a hairline between rows, no card inside a card) and an aside. The group headings
are `heading-2` with no icon or number chip. The rules: the categories are a list in the aside (a row of
chips that jump to them below lg, which a phone had none of), and each row is the rule's title, its id (a
mono line on a phone, beside the title from md) and its severity; the search in the head narrows them as
before. The fix guides and the glossary: the aside is `KnowledgeNav`, the four directories with their
counts and the current one marked (it is how the glossary, which the header does not list, is reached from
its neighbours), and the guides' own two groups above it when there are two (today all 15 guides are in one).

### The 404 page

The 168 px gradient number is a **48 px (56 from lg) solid teal «404»**, a number and not a heading; then the
h1, one line, a search that sends the words to the hub (`?q=`), and four ways on (the home page, the tools,
the rules, the scan) as 44 px buttons. No tint behind but the head's faint one, no card.

### Shared CSS (`global.css`, one block at the end, `/* R7 knowledge */`)

`search-field` (the field's wrapper: 48 px, 52 from lg, a `field` border, an indigo ring on focus),
`kb-list`, `kb-row` and `kb-dot` (a list of links as one card of rows; the row; its dot), `kb-link` (a link
in an aside: 44 px on a phone, 34 from lg, tunable by two variables), `kb-box` (a card without its shadow, for
asides: a sticky aside scrolls inside itself, and a scrolling box clips a shadow with a hard edge) and
`kb-filter` (a row of the hub's filter list). Every colour is a token.

### Copy (`packages/i18n`, all `reviewed: false` as their files are)

New: `knowledge.showAll`, `showFewer`; `rules.page.contents`, `guides`, `terms`; `pages.bot.related` and
`methodology.related`. Changed: `knowledge.types.fix` is «أدلة الإصلاح» / «Fix guides» (it was «إصلاح Search
Console», which broke the group's heading in two lines on a phone and differed from the header's name for the
section); the leads of the hub (English), the rule library, the fix guides, the glossary and the bot's page
are cut to fit **two lines on a phone** (§8: «cut copy to fit»); the 404's line reads «ابحث عمّا تريد، أو اختر
صفحة:» since the search now comes before the links.

## Where R7 (knowledge) departs from §8

1. **The hub's filter is two renderings**: chips (a `scroll-row`) below lg and the vertical list in the aside
   from lg, as the task asked (§8 has only the list). Between md and lg the chips wrap in one line.
2. **The tiles stay**, as four compact tiles at the foot of the hub on every screen, not in the aside: the
   aside is the filter, and the tiles are the way to the directories.
3. **`DocHeading` and `Contents` have no numbers** (R7a's departure 5 kept a small grey one).
4. **A phone gets the contents as a disclosure under the head** and the aside after the text, for the bot's
   page and the methodology too (R7a: contents first); §8 says the aside's parts follow the main content.
5. **Aside boxes have no shadow** (`kb-box`), where §8's card has the soft one: a sticky aside clips it.
6. **One card of related links**, not a box for each kind, and no id line under a related rule: the sticky
   aside has 788 px at 1440×900, and a rule's aside was 863 px at the median with the old boxes (now 731; 10
   of the 61 rules still scroll inside theirs, the largest by 200 px).
7. **The FAQ is a list, not cards.**
8. **Rows show a dot, not a pill, for the category**, as the tools' rows do; the category's name is text from
   md. (A phone's row has no category name: the search still reads it.)
9. **The hub shows five rows of each group and hides the rest** (§8 only asks for a shorter page): the
   disclosure is native and the rows stay in the HTML.
10. **`types.fix` was renamed**, and the leads and the 404's line cut (above): copy changes the task allowed
    («their strings»), for the owner to read.
11. **A shared test changed**: `contrast.browser.test.ts` sampled one pixel past the right and bottom edge of
    a text's box. A line of code clipped to its block ends at the block's edge, and the pixel past it is the
    page's ground: the bot's code block (358 px wide, rounded) put every third column of its first row on
    that edge, 1.28:1, depending only on the block's geometry. The last pixel of a box is the one before its
    far edge.
